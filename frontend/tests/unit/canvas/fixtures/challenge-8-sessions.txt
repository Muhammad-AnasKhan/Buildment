Challenge 8 (a WireGuard VPN): learner sessions from real runs
================================================================

Recorded 2026-10-27 in the lab image (networking-lab), started with --privileged.
The scripts are the reference solutions (every gap filled), on top of the learner template's toolkit.
Every line below "(lab) #" is the exact output of that command. Nothing is invented or edited.
Keys, times, ids, ports chosen by Linux and byte counts differ on every run.

What to find where:
  PART 1: the website's answer for the cafe and the office, the ping that fails, forward-test.txt
  PART 2: ping before and with the tunnel, wg show on both ends, ping with wg0 down, the false source
  PART 3: the cafe outside capture, the wg0 capture, the 156-byte outer packet, the inside plug,
          and the optional keepalive extra
  PART 4: the website's answer before, in the full tunnel and in the split tunnel, the routes,
          the DNS query captures (cafe in the clear, then inside wg0), the split capture
