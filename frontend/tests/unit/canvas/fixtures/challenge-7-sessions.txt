Challenge 7 (BGP): real lab sessions for the chapter writers
=============================================================

Recorded 2026-10-27 in the lab image networking-lab (Ubuntu 24.04, BIRD 2.14), Docker Desktop on Windows, --privileged.
Every line below was copied from a real run. Nothing is invented or edited.
Lines that start with "$ " are what a learner types. The lines under them are the exact output.
Times (16:40:04.307 and so on), ping times and traceroute times differ on every run.

The first block (PARTS 1 to 4) is a learner who filled every gap (the reference solution).
The second block (TEMPLATE) is the learner template: the guided parts' tests run too early or with
something broken, and the gapped parts run with every gap left empty, so each test fails and names the gap.

Where to find things:
  PART 1: birdc show protocols, show route, show route all 203.0.113.0/24, ip route on r1,
          ping that works (with -I) and ping that fails (without -I), r3's view, bird -p, test.
  PART 2: show route all on r1 and r2, the withdrawal (disable direct1), Network not found,
          ip route without the route, "Network is unreachable", failed ping, Import withdraws,
          the return (enable direct1), test.
  PART 3: before and after the preference (the * moves), the cut (disable to_r1 on r2),
          traffic on the backup (via 10.0.13.3, ttl=64), traceroute 2 hops then 1 hop, restore, test.
  PART 4: before the hijack, during (the /25 wins, r2 answers .130, r1 still answers .1),
          after the filter (r1 answers both, the /25 in show route filtered), test.
