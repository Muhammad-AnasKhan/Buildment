# Canvas spec: Dina's first habit: change what matters

## Teaching claim
Choose the habit to start first by what matters now, how small it can be, whether it has a clear anchor and whether it helps the rest of the day; the right first habit changes when what matters this month changes.

## Why a canvas
The chapter works out Dina's totals once. Changing one weight (her tired mornings make "helps the rest of the day" very important) and watching sleep overtake English makes "it depends on your month" concrete. A static table shows one month only.

## Model
- Inputs: one weight per criterion.

  | Name | Unit | Min | Max | Step | Default |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Matters most now | weight level | 1 | 3 | 1 | 3 |
  | Can be very small | weight level | 1 | 3 | 1 | 2 |
  | Has a clear anchor | weight level | 1 | 3 | 1 | 2 |
  | Helps the rest of the day | weight level | 1 | 3 | 1 | 1 |

- Fixed data (Dina's scores, 1 to 5): English 5, 4, 4, 1; Sleep by 23:00 3, 2, 4, 5; 10-minute walk 2, 5, 3, 3.
- Outputs: total points per habit, the leader or a tie, the margin.
- Formula: total = sum of weight x score (shared `renderers/factor/model.ts`).
- Worked values: default English 32, sleep 26, walk 25. Helps at 3: sleep 36, English 34, walk 31. All weights 1: English 14, sleep 14, walk 13 (tie). Matters at 1: English 22, walk 21, sleep 20.
- Assumptions: scores and weights are exact.
- Limitations: Dina's judgement; small gaps mean both are reasonable; the model picks only the first habit.

## Rendering
- The shared factor renderer: weight buttons above, total bars below, the "What changed" sentence from `describeChange`.
- Static fallback: chapter 6.2's worked example table and arithmetic.

## Controls
Three weight levels per criterion (nice to have, important, very important).

## Visualisation
Three total bars; the leader is named in words.

## Copy
- What this shows: in `whatThisShows`.
- Try this:
  1. Helps the rest of the day to very important: sleep first, 36 to 34 (the chapter's predict).
  2. Every weight to 1: English and sleep tie at 14.
  3. Matters most now to 1: English leads the walk by only 1.
- What changed: generated by the shared factor model.
- Limits of the model: as in `limits`.

## Edge cases
Ties are named as ties. Long option names ("10-minute walk", "Sleep by 23:00") fit at 375px.

## Accessibility
Weight buttons at least 44px tall, labelled; totals as text; the change sentence in a live region.
