# Canvas spec: sysdesign-m19-failure-time

- **Course and chapter:** system-design, 19.3 "RPO, RTO, backups and point-in-time recovery", beat 1 ("Pick the moment it fails").
- **Renderer:** custom beat canvas on `BeatFrame`, `TightRailSlider` (the failure marker), `SvgRadioChip` (the three modes, drawn inside the picture), the new `primitives/svg-step-bar.tsx` (`StepBar`: a stacked bar of steps with a key), `VerdictBanner`, `useBeatClock`.
- **Claim:** The data lost is the gap back to the last recovery point, and the time to recover is the sum of copy-back, replay and switch-over. A plan with a log loses less data but can recover more slowly; a live copy in another region does both well and does not protect against a wrong write (19.3 beats 3 and 4).
- **Model:** `model.ts`, unit tested. h = hours since the 02:00 backup, 0 to 24 in steps of 0.5 (default 12). Made-up constants: 1 TB, 250 MB/s restore (copy-back 4,000 s = 67 min), switch 10 min, replay 10 times faster than real time (6 min per hour), log every 5 min, copy lag 1 s, 300 writes a second, promote 1 min. Nightly: lost h hours, back in 77 min. Log: lost at most 5 min, back in 67 + 6h + 10. Copy: lost 1 s, back in 11 min for every h. Tested: h = 12 gives 149 min, h = 24 gives 221 min, h = 0 gives 77 min.
- **Inputs:** drag the marker on the day bar (snaps every half hour; arrow keys move 0.5 h, Page keys 3 h, Home and End the ends); three mode chips ("Backup only", "Backup + log", "Region B copy"); outside the picture: Reset.
- **Motion:** the marker has a soft pulsing halo; the red span (in log and copy mode a short fixed mark beside the marker, wider than 5 minutes or 1 second would be at day scale), the ticks (one every 5 minutes up to the failure, in log mode), the step bar and the verdict change with every drag or tap. Reduced motion draws a still halo.
- **Verdicts:** "12 h lost; back in 77 min" (hours whole when whole, one decimal otherwise), "5 min lost at most; back in 149 min", "1 s lost; back in 11 min", "Nothing lost yet; back in 77 min" (nightly at h = 0). Tested up to 38 characters for every mode and half hour.
- **What to notice:** the nightly red span grows with h but its recovery time does not; the log mode's red span is tiny but its replay step grows with h; the copy mode stays short for any h. The bar is labelled 02:00 at both ends.
- **Deviation from the spec text:** the spec says the stacked bar carries minutes "on the segments"; the minutes sit in a colour-keyed key under the bar instead, because short segments (a 1 minute promote, a 3 minute replay) are too narrow to hold a label. The three chips are drawn inside the picture (`SvgRadioChip`) as the spec's "inside the picture" asks, not as the HTML `BeatSegmented` row.
- **Limits (in the fold):** made-up size, speeds and a nightly backup at 02:00; real restores depend on the storage, the engine and the load, and a drill (19.3 beat 5) is the only way to know yours.
- **Sources:** [S108] RPO and RTO; [S38] replicas typically under a second behind; [S107] Aurora Global Database latency and promotion; Amazon RDS documentation on transaction logs every five minutes (not yet in the dossier).
