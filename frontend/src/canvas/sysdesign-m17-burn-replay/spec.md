# Canvas spec: sysdesign-m17-burn-replay

- **Course and chapter:** system-design, 17.4 ("Error budgets and burn-rate alerts"), beat 3, "Replay an incident, see who is paged". Playable; new slug.
- **Renderer:** custom beat canvas on `BeatFrame`: incident chips (`RadioChipRow`, a radio group of `SvgRadioChip`s) and three alert toggles (`SvgChip`, pressed state) inside the picture, a burn chart, one bar per alert, a budget bar, a time rail (`TightRailSlider`, a real `role="slider"`), `DotGrid`, `VerdictBanner`. One control sits under the picture: a Replay `BeatButton`. `year-strip` was not used (it draws days of a year, this is a minute timeline) and the rows are always three, greyed when off, so the layout does not jump.
- **Claim:** A naive alert pages for every blip. A burn-rate alert with a long and a short window pages for the outage and the slow leak, ignores the blip, and stops soon after a fix.
- **Input:** incident: 3 min blip, 30 min outage, 5 h leak (default outage); three alert toggles, all on; the time rail over the axis (arrow keys step 1 minute, Page keys 10, Home and End the ends), default the end of the axis. Replay walks the rail from minute 0 to the end in 7 seconds (it jumps to the end under reduced motion); touching the rail stops it.
- **Model** (`model.ts`, fluid, no noise): SLO 99.9% (allowance 0.1%), baseline 0.05%; the average rate over (x - W, x] is b + (r - b) x overlap / W; burn = average / 0.1%. Fast fires when the 1 h and 5 min burns are both at or above 14.4; slow when the 6 h and 30 min burns are both at or above 6; naive when the 5 min burn is at or above 1. Incidents: blip 3 min at 8%, outage 30 min at 5%, leak 300 min at 1.2%. Budget = 0.001 x 30 x 1,440 = 43.2 bad minutes; bad minutes = rate x minutes of incident so far.
- **Numbers to check** (tested; whole minutes after the incident starts): outage, fast pages at 17 and clears at 34 (2.0% of budget used); naive pages at 1 and clears at 35. Leak, slow pages at 173 and clears at 316 (4.8%); naive clears at 305. Blip, naive clears at 8; fast and slow never. Outage slow and leak fast never page. Peak 1 hour burn: blip 4.5, outage 25.3, leak 12.0. Peak 6 hour burn: blip 1.2, outage 4.6, leak 10.1.
- **Changes to the first spec:** (1) The naive alert pages at "minute 1", not "under 1 min": time is sampled in whole minutes, and the bad minutes at the page are counted at that minute (0.85 for the outage fast page, where the first spec had 0.84 from a continuous crossing; both are 2.0% of the budget). (2) The rail text reads "minute 17, 1 hour burn 14.5, ...": at exactly minute 17 the burn is 14.5, and 14.4 is where it crosses the line a little earlier (minute 16.85). (3) The alert rows stay on screen, drawn dashed and empty when the alert is off, instead of disappearing.
- **Picture:** top left a legend; a chart of the 1 hour burn (blue) and 6 hour burn (violet) with the incident shaded and dashed lines at 14.4 and 6; the curves are solid up to the rail and faint after it, with dots at the rail. Under it one row per alert: a toggle, its rule in words, a bar on the same time axis that fills red while the alert fires, a heavy red tick where it pages, and a line "paged at minute 17, 2% of budget used" (or "quiet so far", or "never pages"). The naive row carries the fixed caption "plus about 27 false pages a day, challenge 16". Then the budget bar, drained as the rail moves, and the time rail.
- **Verdicts:** naive on and blip: "Paged for a 3 minute blip" (bad); blip with no alert paging: "Blip ignored; budget barely touched" (good); outage with fast on: "Paged at minute 17; 2% of budget gone" (good); leak with slow on: "Leak found at minute 173; 4.8% gone" (good); leak without slow and only fast on: "The slow leak was never paged" (bad); outage without fast and only slow on: "The outage was never paged" (bad). Extra: outage or leak where only the naive alert pages: "Paged at once, and for every blip" (warn).
- **Reduced motion:** the curves and bars are drawn for the whole axis from the first frame (nothing depends on a clock); the rail still moves; Replay jumps to the end.
- **Accessibility:** chips are buttons with pressed state (incidents are a radio group); the rail is a slider with value text such as "minute 17, 1 hour burn 14.4, fast alert firing"; a polite live region lists the alerts firing and the verdict; the SVG description gives the burn rates, each alert's page minute and the budget used.
- **Sources:** multiwindow, multi-burn-rate alerts with 14.4 (1 h and 5 min, 2% of budget) and 6 (6 h and 30 min, 5% of budget) (Google SRE Workbook, "Alerting on SLOs", source needed in the dossier); error budget [S96]. The incidents and the baseline are course numbers.
