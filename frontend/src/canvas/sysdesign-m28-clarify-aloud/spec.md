# Canvas spec: sysdesign-m28-clarify-aloud

- **Course and chapter:** system-design, 28.2 beat 2 ("Clarify with four short questions").
- **Renderer:** `scene` (data only).
- **Claim:** Four short questions (who, how many, what is out, how fast) fix the scope in minutes, and writing the answers on the board makes them visible to the interviewer.
- **Story:** The candidate asks four questions in turn. The interviewer answers "public links", "500M a month", "no accounts" and "under 20 ms". The candidate writes them on the board. Verdict: "Four answers fix the scope".
- **Numbers:** The Wasla scope from chapter 20.1: 500 million new links a month, p99 under 20 ms at the service.
- **Sources:** Chapter 20.1 (course numbers); chapter 1.2 for the three kinds of requirement.
