# Canvas spec: sysdesign-m19-fail-region

- **Course and chapter:** system-design, 19.4 "Active-passive and active-active", beat 2 ("Fail a region, compare the four").
- **Renderer:** custom beat canvas on `BeatFrame`, `SnapStopsRail` (the ladder), `SvgButton` (Region A), the new `primitives/svg-step-bar.tsx` (`StepBar`) and `primitives/svg-info-chip.tsx` (`InfoChip`), `VerdictBanner`, `useBeatClock`.
- **Claim:** Down the ladder from backup and restore to active-active, downtime and lost writes fall and cost rises; active-active removes the failover step and adds write conflicts.
- **Model:** `model.ts`, unit tested. Made-up steps: decide 10 min, rebuild 30, restore 67 (19.3), promote 1 [S107], start the app 10, move users 2.5 (health check 30 s x 3 plus a 60 s TTL, 19.5); 300 writes a second, 1 s copy lag. Backup and restore: decide + rebuild + restore + move = 109.5 min (about 110), up to 1 h of writes lost, cost 1.1. Pilot light: 10 + 1 + 10 + 2.5 = 23.5 (about 24), 300 writes, 1.2. Warm standby: 10 + 1 + 2.5 = 13.5 (about 14), 300 writes, 1.5. Active-active: move only, 2.5 (about 3) for A's half of the users, 150 writes at risk, 2.0, with the standing note "Same record written in both: one edit is lost."
- **Inputs:** a four stop ladder (default warm standby; arrow keys, Home, End); tap the Region A card (a real button) to fail it and tap again to bring it back; outside the picture: Reset. Changing the plan after a failure re-runs it.
- **Motion:** after the tap Region A turns red, the step bar fills left to right over 3.5 seconds (unit length does not follow the minutes), the users on A turn grey while it fills and then glide to green on Region B; a "writes lost" chip appears; Region B's pill goes standby, moving, serving. What runs in Region B (backups only, live copy and no app, live copy and a small app, live copy and a full app) is drawn as two icon tiles. The cost bar always shows the multiple. Reduced motion shows the finished state.
- **Verdicts:** "Pick a plan, then fail Region A", "Down about 110 min; up to 1 h lost", "Down about 24 min; 300 writes lost", "Down about 14 min; 300 writes lost", "A's users back in 3 min; 150 at risk" (all within 38 characters, tested).
- **What to notice:** the biggest drop in downtime is the first step; the biggest jump in cost is the last; active-active has no decision or promotion step and half the users never notice.
- **Deviation from the spec text:** step minutes sit in a colour-keyed key under the bar (some segments are too narrow for a label), and the key appears only after Region A fails.
- **Limits (in the fold):** made-up step durations, write rate and costs; the order of the rungs comes from the AWS guide, the numbers do not.
- **Sources:** [S107] the four plans, Aurora promotion under a minute, lag under a second; [S108] RTO and RPO; chapter 19.5 for the 2.5 minute DNS move.
