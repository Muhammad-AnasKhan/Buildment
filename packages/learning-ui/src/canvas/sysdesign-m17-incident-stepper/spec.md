# Canvas spec: sysdesign-m17-incident-stepper

- **Course and chapter:** system-design, 17.6 ("Incidents and postmortems"), beat 2, "Step through an incident". Playable; new slug.
- **Renderer:** custom beat canvas on `BeatFrame`: an `SvgNode` clock card, a small error-rate chart drawn in the picture, two full-width choice pills (a local `ChoiceChip` modelled on `SvgChip`, 44 units to tap, with a tried state), `DotGrid`, `VerdictBanner`. `design-stepper` was not used: it grows a design one step at a time with Back and Next, while this is a choose-one-of-two walk where a wrong pick stays on the step. A `BeatButton` "Start over" sits under the picture.
- **Claim:** In an incident each next move has a better and a worse choice, and the better ones are mostly about order: tell people, give one person the lead, undo the change before hunting the bug, confirm it is over, and learn without blame.
- **Input:** five steps, two choices each. The better choice moves on. The worse choice stays on the step, is shown struck off, shows its cost in the verdict banner and adds 5 minutes to the clock. The better choice is not always the top one (top, bottom, top, bottom, top).
- **Script** (`model.ts`, course numbers): 19:27 acknowledge and tell the channel (or wait five minutes); 19:29 one lead and one hand on the system (or everyone tries fixes); 19:32 roll back to v41 (or read the code first); 19:36 watch 15 minutes then post the all-clear at 19:51 (or close at once); next day a blameless postmortem (or find who wrote the line).
- **Chart:** error rate 0% to 19:20, 6% from 19:20, 0% from 19:36. Each wrong pick moves the clock, the rollback, the point where errors end and the all-clear by 5 minutes (a wrong pick in step 4 or 5 moves only what comes after it). The rollback flag (19:33) appears after step 3 and the all-clear flag (19:51) after step 4. The axis is 19:15 to 20:20 so five wrong picks still fit.
- **Verdicts:** "Page at 19:27: choose a move" at the start; after each step "Acknowledged; the team knows", "One lead, one hand on the system", "Rolled back: errors fall to 0%", "Verified before the all-clear", "Postmortem about systems, not people"; a wrong pick shows its cost ("Two changes collided; +5 min", and four more); finish: "Errors gone 9 minutes after the page" with no wrong pick, otherwise "Resolved, but wrong picks cost time".
- **Reduced motion:** the chart is drawn at its final state for each step; new flags and the green segment appear with a CSS fade that is off under reduced motion.
- **Accessibility:** the choices are buttons with text (a tried choice is `aria-disabled` and says what it cost); the verdict and clock are in a polite live region; the SVG description gives the step, the clock and when the errors began and ended.
- **Sources:** declare early, clear roles and a living incident document (Google SRE book, "Managing Incidents", source needed in the dossier); blameless postmortem (Google SRE book, "Postmortem Culture", source needed in the dossier). The story is a course composite.
