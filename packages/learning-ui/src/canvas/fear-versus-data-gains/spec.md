# Canvas spec: Gains depend on the task

## Teaching claim
In a 2023 study of 758 consultants, AI users did more tasks, faster and better, on tasks inside what the tool did well, and were less often right on one task outside it. The answer depends on the task (dossier A35).

## Why a canvas
Two tasks, one tool, two results: a story with the consultant, the AI help and the result shows the contrast in one picture.

## Model
- Story: the consultant sends a "task it suits" to the AI help; a draft goes to the result, which says "more, faster". Then a "task it misses"; the result says "less accurate". Verdict: "It depends on the task".
- Numbers: none on the picture. The chapter fold gives the study's figures (12.2% more tasks, 25.1% faster, over 40% higher quality inside; 19 percentage points less likely correct on one task outside).
- Limits: one study, one firm, GPT-4 in 2023, tasks designed by the researchers, one task outside.
