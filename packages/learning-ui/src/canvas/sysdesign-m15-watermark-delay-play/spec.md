# Canvas spec: sysdesign-m15-watermark-delay-play

- **Course and chapter:** system-design, 15.6 ("Stream processing: windows, watermarks and late events"), beat 4, "Drag the delay: fast or complete". Playable; new slug.
- **Renderer:** custom beat canvas on `BeatFrame`: `SnapStopsRail` for the delay (inside the picture), `SvgNode` cards (Result, Counted, Dropped or Corrected), a plot of the 40 events, two window bars, `DotGrid`, `VerdictBanner`. One toggle outside the picture ("Allowed lateness", `BeatChoice`: none or 30 s). `picture-slider.tsx` and `log-ruler.tsx` were not used: the delay has seven fixed stops and needs a snap rail, and the axis is linear.
- **Claim:** A short watermark delay closes windows soon, so results come fast, but late events find the window closed and are dropped. A long delay keeps more events and delays every result. No setting is both fast and complete.
- **Input:** delay 0, 5, 10, 20, 30, 45 or 60 s on a snap rail, spaced by value (default 10; arrows move to the next stop). Toggle: allowed lateness none (default) or 30 s.
- **Model** (`model.ts`, `evaluate`): 40 events, tumbling windows of 60 s, the event list of the spec. The watermark is the newest event time seen so far minus the delay, read before each event; an event is dropped when the watermark is at or past its window's end plus the allowed lateness. With the allowed lateness, an event that finds the window closed but is within the lateness is "corrected" (a late correction).
- **Numbers to check** (tested): no allowed lateness: delay 0 drops 4 (90% counted), 5 drops 1 (97.5%), 10 drops 1, and 20, 30, 45, 60 drop none. Dropped at delay 0: the events at 48, 54, 51 and 27 s; at 5 and 10: the one at 27 s (50 s late). The four slowest events arrive 60, 52, 50 and 15.9 s late. With the 30 s lateness nothing is dropped at any delay; corrected: 4, 1, 1 and then none.
- **Picture:** each event is a dot placed by event time (across) and by how late it arrived (up), so the stragglers sit high; dropped dots are red with a cross, corrected dots amber; two bars under the axis give each window's "16 of 20 counted"; the watermark is a dashed line that moves right as events arrive, and the event that has just arrived gets a ring; the Result card says how far behind the results run ("10 s behind"). The delay rail is in the picture.
- **Verdicts:** "4 events dropped, results instant" (warn), "1 dropped, results 10 s behind" (warn), "Nothing dropped, results 30 s late" (good); with lateness on, "4 corrected later, results instant" or "1 corrected later, 10 s behind" (good).
- **Reduced motion:** the final state is drawn at once: the watermark rests at its last position and no ring shows.
- **Accessibility:** the rail value text reads "delay 10 seconds, 1 event dropped of 40, results 10 seconds behind"; every dot has a title with its event time, lateness and fate; the SVG description gives both windows' counts.
- **Sources:** Flink windows and allowed lateness [S87]; Flink time concepts page (source note in chapter 15.6). The events are a course example.
