# Canvas spec: product-m2-three-sizes

- **Course and chapter:** product-sense, 2.2 "One thing first: visual hierarchy", the third beat (an extra canvas).
- **Renderer:** custom, on the reusable specimen compare beat. This folder holds only data. Reason: "no more than three sizes" is easier to believe when the learner switches between one, three and six sizes of the same page.
- **Claim:** With one size nothing comes first. With three sizes (a large title, a medium detail size, a small note) the order of the lines is clear. With six sizes the sizes compete and the eye has no order to follow.
- **Model:** one Halaqa group page (title, place, time, seats left, a one-line description, host, a Join button) in three versions. 1 size: every line at 14, one weight. 3 sizes: title 22 bold, details and description 16, host note 13. 6 sizes: 28, 21, 18, 15, 13 and 11. The words are the same in the three. In "1 size" every line also has one weight and one colour, so size is the only signal missing; in the other two the title is bold, the seats line is semibold and the details are grey. Verdicts: "Flat: no order" (bad), "The order is clear" (good), "Noisy: sizes compete" (warn).
- **Inputs:** three pills (1 size, 3 sizes, 6 sizes).
- **What changes:** the screen and the verdict.
- **What the learner should notice:** order comes from a few clearly different steps, not from many small differences.
- **Accessibility:** the pills form a labelled group; a polite live region reads each version and verdict; the SVG has a title; text at least 11 units.
- **Limits (fold):** the three-size guide comes from one source and a long page may need more steps; sizes are drawn units, not pixels; Halaqa and its people are fictional.
- **Sources:** NN/g on visual hierarchy, no more than three sizes [A69].
