# Canvas spec: sysdesign-m15-retry-reorder-play

- **Course and chapter:** system-design, 15.4 ("Delivery, ordering and partition keys"), beat 3, "A retry can swap two events". Playable; new slug.
- **Renderer:** custom beat canvas on `BeatFrame`: `SvgNode` cards for the producer, the broker log and the consumer, three lanes on one millisecond axis, `DotGrid`, `VerdictBanner`. Two controls outside the picture: a toggle ("First send is lost", `BeatButton`) and a three-way `BeatSegmented` ("Send freely", "One at a time", "Version numbers"). `log-ruler.tsx` was not used: it is a log-scale ruler of named rungs, and this axis is linear in milliseconds.
- **Claim:** If two updates for one key are in flight and the first fails and is retried, the log holds them in the wrong order and the final state is wrong. Waiting for each ack, or numbering each update so the consumer can skip old ones, keeps the state right.
- **Input:** toggle (default on) and segmented control (default send freely).
- **Model** (`model.ts`, `run`): update 1, "paid", is sent at 0 ms; update 2, "refunded", at 10 ms. A write lands 5 ms after a send and its ack reaches the producer 5 ms later; the timeout is 50 ms. A lost first send is sent again at 50 ms and written at 55 ms. One at a time: update 2 is sent when update 1 is acked. Version numbers: the consumer keeps the highest version applied and skips older ones.
- **Numbers to check** (tested): free and lost: log 2, 1; final paid (wrong); 55 ms. Free and not lost: log 1, 2; refunded; 15 ms. One at a time and lost: update 1 written at 55 and acked at 60, update 2 sent at 60 and written at 65; log 1, 2; refunded; 65 ms (4.3 times 15). Version numbers and lost: log 2, 1; update 1 skipped; refunded; 55 ms.
- **Picture:** the producer lane shows numbered sends (the retry on an amber ring with a "retry" pill) and the producer's wait as a bracket; the lost send is a dashed red line ending in a cross; lines run to the log lane, where each write is a square; a line drops from each write to the consumer lane, where the update is applied (a skipped one is dashed and says "skipped"). Under the lanes, the log as boxes in the order written, and the total time. One dot flies along each message in turn; reduced motion shows no dots.
- **Verdicts:** "Order shows paid, not refunded" (bad), "Order holds, nothing failed" (good, whenever nothing is lost), "Safe, but 65 ms instead of 15" (warn), "Safe: the old version is skipped" (good).
- **Reduced motion:** no flying dots; the final picture shows both boxes in the log and the consumer's status.
- **Accessibility:** the verdict and the card statuses are text; the SVG description spells out the log order and the final state; the toggle is a native button with `aria-pressed` and the segmented control is native radio buttons.
- **Sources:** Kafka idempotent producer and sequence numbers [S82]; the ordering effect of retries is course reasoning (see the source note in chapter 15.4).
