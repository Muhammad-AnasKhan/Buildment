# Canvas spec: product-m4-four-levels

- **Course and chapter:** product-sense, 4.2 "Average, good, best, remarkable", beat 3 ("Place each claim on the scale").
- **Renderer:** custom, on the reusable `Sorter` primitive (`primitives/sorter.tsx`, model `models/sorter.ts`) with four buckets. This folder holds only data. Reason: the learner must decide and get a reason for each wrong try, which a diagram cannot do.
- **Claim:** The course's four-level scale has checks at every level, and each level needs every check below it. A screen goes at the highest level whose checks the stated facts show are met. The top level needs a named choice and a reason a reader can name.
- **Model:** nine cards, each a short statement of facts about a made-up screen, dealt in data order and in a seeded shuffle after "Sort again". Buckets: Average (works, defaults kept), Good (passes the standards), Best (clear, tested, fixed), Remarkable (a named choice). A right bucket flies the card in. A wrong bucket shakes and shows a reason written for that card and that bucket.
- **Cards (9):** Average: works with every default unchanged; polished but pale hint text fails contrast; every Good check passes except a blank error state. Good: all Good standards pass and nobody has tried it; Good checks pass but three things compete when blurred. Best: Good checks pass, spacing steady, extras cut, one thing leads and 4 of 5 finished after fixes; Best checks pass with a bright orange header and no reason (the trap that a choice without a reason stays at Best). Remarkable: Best checks pass and seats left is the largest text because seats decide the join; Best checks pass and rooms open on today's free hours because students book the same day.
- **Inputs:** tap a bucket (a real button over each tray) or drag the card onto a tray.
- **What changes:** bucket counts, the card, and the reason shown after a wrong try.
- **What the learner should notice:** one failed check keeps a screen at the level below, looks are not a check, and a choice needs a reason a reader can name.
- **Accessibility:** tray buttons are labelled with the name, gloss and count; a polite live region reads each result and reason; text at least 11.5 units on a 420 unit view box; every reason fits two lines of 60 characters and every card fits three lines of 40.
- **Limits (in the data):** the scale is the course's own and not an industry standard. The canvas checks that a Remarkable card states a choice and a reason. It cannot say whether the choice is right for the job; the learner defends that in words, and no graded item in the module keys Remarkable.
- **Sources:** the level checks in the chapter's fold [A1, A2, A4, A5, A8, A11, A16, A20, A21, A22, A23, A38, A39, A49, A58, A67, A69, A90]; the scale itself is the course's own method.
