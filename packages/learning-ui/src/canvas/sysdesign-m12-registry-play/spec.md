# Canvas spec: sysdesign-m12-registry-play

- **Course and chapter:** system-design, 12.9 ("Service discovery and coordination services"), beat 2 "Register, beat, expire, watch". New slug.
- **Renderer:** custom beat canvas on `BeatFrame`: `SvgNode` cards for payments-1 to payments-3, a time strip per instance (beats as ticks, the lease as a bar that shrinks to the blue "now" line, the stale span hatched red), a registry card and a watcher card, `SvgPillButton` for "Crash" and "Lose beat", two `RailSlider` rails (lease, beat interval) drawn with `SliderRail`, `VerdictBanner`. "Pause the clock" and "Reset" are the two controls under the picture.
- **Claim:** A crashed instance stays in the registry until its lease runs out, so callers can be sent to it for up to a lease length. A lease shorter than about three beat intervals removes healthy instances when beats are lost.
- **Model** (`models/registry-lease.ts`, a pure function of config, events and time): instances register at t = 0 and beat every I seconds from offsets 0, 1 and 2 s. A beat that arrives before the key's expiry sets the expiry to that moment plus the lease; a beat at or after the expiry finds the key deleted and registers it again. A key is deleted when the clock reaches its expiry; the watcher is told 0.01 s later. "Lose beat" loses the next beat that has not been sent; pressing it again loses the one after. "Crash" stops all later beats. Events are stored to a tenth of a second.
- **Formulas:** stale window after a crash = expiry - crash time, between TTL - I and TTL; average TTL - I / 2; lost beats in a row survived = max(0, ceil(TTL / I) - 2). Unit tests check every row of the chapter's table against the model (a crash at every hundredth of a second between two beats; n and n + 1 lost beats).
- **Inputs:** lease on a snap rail 4, 6, 8, 10, 15, 30 s (default 10); beat interval on a snap rail 1, 2, 3, 5 s (default 3). Moving a rail starts the run again. The clock runs 1 s of model time per 0.4 s of real time, pauses off screen and on the Pause button.
- **Worked example (default):** crash payments-1 at t = 4 right after its beat at 3: listed until 13, "Dead one listed for 9 s more" counting down, then "Gone after 9 s; watcher told". Lose the beats at 3 and 6: the beat at 9 arrives before 10, "Survived 2 lost beats". Lose 3, 6 and 9: the key expires at 10, "Healthy one expired: lease too short".
- **Verdicts (38 characters or fewer):** "Survives 2 lost beats in a row" (at the start and after a rail move), "Dead one listed for 9 s more", "Gone after 9 s; watcher told", "Healthy one expired: lease too short", "Survived 2 lost beats", "payments-1 will lose a beat".
- **Reduced motion:** the clock moves in whole seconds (one step per 0.4 s); nothing slides.
- **Accessibility:** rails are `role="slider"` with value text "lease 10 s, beat every 3 s: survives 2 lost beats"; the crash and lose-beat buttons are labelled per instance ("Crash payments-2"); a live region lists the verdict, the registry and the last watcher events as text.
- **Sources:** etcd leases, transactions and watches [S53, S54]. The beat and lease figures are course numbers.
