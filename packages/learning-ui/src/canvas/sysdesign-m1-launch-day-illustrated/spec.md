# Canvas spec: sysdesign-m1-launch-day-illustrated

## Visual direction

- **Style:** Playful, warm, object-first illustration inspired by Brilliant.org and Duolingo. Instead of white rectangular cards on a grey dot grid, the picture is a cohesive storybook scene with a warm cream-and-apricot sky in light mode, a twilight plum sky in dark mode, a grounded floor deck, and a terraced green campus lawn.
- **Student crowd and visible queue (left):** A wooden/enamel campus signpost shows the live student count (`1,000 online`). On the sandy queue walkway below, up to 7 large student avatars (varied skin tones, hairstyles, caps, and hoodies) stand in line facing the server, backed by an amphitheater crowd that scales logarithmically from 3 figures (at 10 students) to 56 figures (at 100,000 students). Their faces and postures react to the load:
  - Below 70% busy: smiling eyes and calm expressions (`Calm: pages load fast`).
  - From 70% to 100% busy: furrowed brows, sweat drops, and floating clock bubbles (`Impatient: pages slow`).
  - At or past 100% busy: frustrated `X` eyes, red timeout badges, and students at the back turning around with dust puffs at their heels to walk away (`Frustrated: giving up`).
- **Flying paper planes and waiting mail chute (center):** Folded paper airplanes and wax-sealed request envelopes glide along an arched flight path into a brass-railed waiting chute in front of the server gate. Waiting envelopes stack in up to 3 rows (`no line`, `waiting line`, `line overflows`). Past 100% capacity, the bottom trapdoor swings open on hinges and red-stamped timeout envelopes tumble out (`timed out`).
- **3D Server rack (right):** A chunky metallic server cabinet on rubber feet holds three bays (`Server`, `App, DB, PDFs`, and a glowing LCD readout for `50% busy`) plus a side glass mercury thermometer. A cooling fan spins at the service rate and status LEDs blink. Near capacity, a warm amber glow surrounds the rack; past 100% capacity, the rack shakes, a roof siren flashes red, and cartoon smoke clouds billow from the top vents.
- **Clipboard knee chart and tactile slider (bottom):** A clipboard chart, hanging on a short cord from the waiting chute, plots the M/M/1 response time curve (`Time per request`) with word axis labels (`idle`, `busy`, `limit`) and the live readout beside the riding orb (`20 ms` or `line grows 400/s`). Along the bottom floor, a recessed track and chunky 3D dial let the learner drag from `few` to `crowd` (`students online`), keeping at most 3 numbers visible in the entire picture at once.

## Model, interactions, and accessibility

- **Model (`model.ts` + `models/server-load.ts`):** Preserves the exact M/M/1 numbers: `0.05` requests/s per student, `10 ms` service time (`100` requests/s capacity), `busy = students * 0.05 / 100`, `timeMs = 10 / (1 - busy)`, and `growthPerSec = arrivals - 100` past capacity.
- **Controls:** Full-surface pointer/touch drag (`PictureSlider`, `touch-none`) snapping to `100`, `1,000`, `2,000` (the one "full" mark) and `10,000` on the `10` to `100,000` log scale, plus keyboard navigation (`Arrow`, `PageUp`/`PageDown`, `Home`, `End`) along `STUDENT_LADDER`.
- **Accessibility and motion:** Keeps `role="slider"`, `aria-valuenow`, `aria-valuetext` (for example, `1,900 students: 95% busy, 200 ms. Slower: requests wait in line`), `<title>` and `<desc>` on the SVG, and `useBeatClock` for `prefers-reduced-motion` and off-screen pausing. Wrapped in `BeatFrame`.

## Label placement and colour

- **No label touches the art.** Every label sits in clear space, the way the card-style version (`sysdesign-m1-launch-day`) places them. The rack is wide enough that `Server`, `App, DB, PDFs` and `5,000% busy` each fit inside their bay, with the LEDs moved clear of the title. The curve readout sits above and left of the riding dot (the curve only rises to the right, so that space is always empty); past 100% it parks top left of the capacity wall, never on the wall or the red band. The clipboard cord runs from the chute to the clip, so no line crosses the chart's title or readout. `timed out` sits below the falling envelopes, between the lawn and the clipboard. The slider's `full` mark sits between the floor's horizon and the handle's halo, with a halo in the floor colour. The curve's right axis word is `limit`, so `full` appears only once.
- **Theme-safe colour.** No hex values: every colour is a Tailwind class (`fill-*`, `stroke-*`, `text-*` with `dark:` variants) or a token from `primitives/tones.ts`. Gradient stops use `stopColor="currentColor"` with a `text-*` class. Light mode is a warm cream sky with a lime lawn; dark mode is a slate-to-indigo night sky with an emerald lawn. Skin, hair, hoodies, envelopes and the metal rack keep one colour in both themes; paper, ink, ground and outlines switch.
- **Checked with** the text-collision helper at 375 px wide, at 10, 100, 1,000, 1,800, 1,900, 10,000 and 100,000 students, in light and dark mode.
