# Canvas spec: sysdesign-m12-fencing-play

- **Course and chapter:** system-design, 12.4 ("Leader election, leases and fencing tokens"), beat 3 "Turn on fencing, drag the pause". New slug.
- **Renderer:** custom beat canvas on `BeatFrame`: `DotGrid`, `VerdictBanner`, `SvgNode` for storage, a 0 to 24 s timeline drawn in SVG, `SliderRail` on `TightRailSlider` for the pause, a small `SvgSwitch` (role="switch") for fencing.
- **Claim:** A lease cannot stop a paused leader that wakes after its lease ended. A fencing token that storage checks stops its write, whatever the length of the pause.
- **Model** (`models/fencing-play.ts`): lease 10 s. A takes the lock at t = 0 with token 33 and freezes at t = 2 s. The lease ends at 10 s. B takes the lock at 10.5 s with token 34 and writes x = B at 11 s. A wakes at 2 + P and writes x = A with token 33 at once. Storage with fencing off accepts every write in arrival order; with fencing on it keeps the highest token seen and rejects a lower one. A tie at 11 s goes to B first.
- **Inputs:** pause P on a rail from 0 to 20 s, step 0.5 s, snap marks at 2, 5, 8, 8.5, 9, 12 and 14 (default 14). Arrow keys step 0.5 s, Page keys 5 s, Home and End jump to the ends. A fencing switch, Off or On (default Off).
- **Outcome by pause:** up to 8 s A writes inside its lease (fine); above 8 and under 9 s the lease is over but B has not written, so A's write lands and then B's (same with fencing, token 33 is still the highest seen); from 9 s A writes after B: fencing off, A overwrites B; fencing on, A is rejected and x = B.
- **Picture:** A's lease bar (blue for the 2 s A ran, grey after the freeze), A's frozen span 2 to 2 + P as a hatched bar with a "paused N s" pill when wide enough, B's lease bar from 10.5 s, the two write markers (B at 11 s, A at 2 + P), a dashed line where A's lease ends. Below, a chip per write in arrival order with a check or a cross, a link to the storage card that ends in a red cross for a rejected write, the card's value and a pill ("highest token 34" with fencing on, "no token check" off). Ambient motion: a dot carries each write to storage and a rejected one turns back at the cross; with reduced motion the chips, links and crosses alone show the result.
- **Verdicts:** "A woke inside its lease: all fine" (good), "Late, but B had not written yet" (warn), "A overwrote B's newer write" (bad), "Token 33 rejected: B's write stands" (good).
- **Checks:** unit tests for pauses 0, 5, 8, 8.5, 9, 14 and 20 with fencing off and on, the tie at 9 s, key steps and the snap marks.
- **Accessibility:** the rail is a slider ("pause 14 s, A wakes at 16 s"); the switch is a real role="switch"; the verdict is a live region.
- **Sources:** fencing token and the 33 and 34 example [S70]; Chubby sequencers [S71]. Lease and pause lengths are course numbers.
