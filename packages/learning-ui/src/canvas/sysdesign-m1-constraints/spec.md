# Canvas spec: sysdesign-m1-constraints

- **Course and chapter:** system-design, 1.2 beat 3 ("Constraints: the walls you build inside").
- **Renderer:** `scene` (data only).
- **Claim:** Constraints are fixed facts about the team, time, money and environment. They remove options before any design work, which saves time.
- **Story:** Hana's team sends three ideas to the constraints box (2 people, 6 weeks). "12 services" gets "2 people: no"; "own search engine" gets "6 weeks: no"; "1 app + Postgres" gets "fits" and moves to the plan. Verdict: "Constraints remove options early".
- **Numbers:** 2 engineers and 6 weeks are the fictional team's limits.
- **Sources:** Fowler, "Monolith First" [S36], cited in the chapter's fold for why a two-person team should not start with many services.
