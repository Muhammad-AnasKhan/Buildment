# Canvas spec: sysdesign-m15-slow-subscriber-play

- **Course and chapter:** system-design, 15.7 ("Publish and subscribe, and fan-out"), beat 2, "One slow subscriber, two designs". Playable; new slug.
- **Renderer:** custom beat canvas on `BeatFrame`: `SvgNode` cards for the order service, the three subscribers and (in the queue design) a queue in front of each, `LinkLine` links with request dots from `flowDots`, `SnapStopsRail` for Receipts' speed (inside the picture), `DotGrid`, `VerdictBanner`. One toggle outside the picture ("Call each in turn" or "A queue for each", `BeatChoice`, default queues). `traffic-playground.tsx` was not used: its picture is one source and one server with an inset curve, not a fan-out to three subscribers.
- **Claim:** If the order service calls every subscriber in turn, one slow subscriber slows every order. With a queue for each subscriber, orders stay fast and only the slow subscriber's queue grows.
- **Input:** Receipts' speed 2, 4, 6, 10, 20, 30 events a second on a snap rail spaced by value (default 6; arrows move to the next stop). Toggle: call each in turn, or a queue for each.
- **Model** (`model.ts`): 20 orders a second. Email and Stats need 33.3 ms per event (30 a second); Receipts needs 1,000 / r ms. Pool of 5 workers. Direct: time per order = 33.3 + 33.3 + 1,000 / r ms; capacity = 5 x 1,000 / that; below 20 the order service falls behind. Queues: an order takes 5 ms; a subscriber's backlog after 600 s = max(0, 20 - speed) x 600.
- **Numbers to check** (tested): Receipts at 30, 20, 10, 6, 4, 2 gives time per order 100, 117, 167, 233, 317, 567 ms, capacity 50.0, 42.9, 30.0, 21.4, 15.8 (behind), 8.8 (behind) a second; the Receipts queue after 10 minutes holds 0, 0, 6,000, 8,400, 9,600, 10,800. Email and Stats hold 0 in every row. Direct and behind: orders waiting after 10 minutes = (20 - capacity) x 600, which is 2,526 at speed 4 and 6,706 at speed 2.
- **Picture:** orders on the left, the three subscribers on the right. Direct: a link to each subscriber with the time of that call on a pill, the order card's pill says "233 ms each" (or "2,526 waiting" when it falls behind), the subscribers each receive less when the order service is behind. Queues: a queue card in front of each subscriber with its count ("8,400 waiting" for Receipts, "empty" for the others) and 5 ms per order. Two number cards: what an order takes, and what the pool can place (direct) or how many wait in Receipts' queue (queues). The "after 10 minutes" label is on the picture.
- **Verdicts:** "Orders slow: 233 ms each" (warn), "Orders fall behind: only 16 a second" (bad), "Orders fast; only Receipts waits" (good), "Everyone keeps up" (good, Receipts at 20 or more).
- **Reduced motion:** no dots; the counts are the same (they are final counts already).
- **Accessibility:** counts are text on the cards; the toggle is a native button group with visible labels; the rail is a `role="slider"` whose value text gives the design, the time per order and the capacity or backlog.
- **Sources:** SNS to SQS fan-out page (source note in chapter 15.7) and Little's law [S5]. The speeds and the pool of 5 are course numbers.
