# Canvas spec: product-m2-front-load

- **Course and chapter:** product-sense, 2.2 "One thing first: visual hierarchy", the fourth beat (an extra canvas).
- **Renderer:** custom, on the reusable specimen compare beat. This folder holds only data. Reason: the F-shaped scan is what readers fall into when a page gives no cues, and the fix (key point first, informative headings, extras cut) is easiest to see on two versions of one page. It replaces a diagram that only restated the finding.
- **Claim:** On a text-heavy page with no cues, readers skim the first words and miss what is buried. Putting the key facts first and starting each line with the informative words lets a skimming reader catch them.
- **Model:** one Halaqa group page in two versions. Buried: the heading "About this study group" (18 bold), one paragraph of about 50 words with the time, place and seats at the end, and a Join button. Up front: the heading is the group's name (20 bold), then the time and place (16), then "2 seats left" (16 semibold), then one short sentence (14 grey), and the Join button. The long welcome is cut. Verdicts: "The key facts come last" (bad), "The key facts come first" (good).
- **Inputs:** two buttons (Buried, Up front).
- **What changes:** the words and order of the page, and the verdict.
- **What the learner should notice:** reading only the first words of each line, the second page tells you the group, the time and the seats; the first tells you nothing.
- **Accessibility:** the two buttons form a labelled group; a polite live region reads each version and verdict; the SVG has a title; text at least 14 units.
- **Limits (fold):** two pages cannot show how a real reader scans; the advice is a guideline from NN/g, not a test result; the group and its words are fictional.
- **Sources:** NN/g on the F-shaped pattern and on front-loading key points [A68]; NN/g heuristic 8, relevant information only [A67].
