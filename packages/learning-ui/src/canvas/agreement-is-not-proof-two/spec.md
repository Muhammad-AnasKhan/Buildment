# Canvas spec: agreement-is-not-proof-two

- **Course and chapter:** build-with-ai (v2), 5.3 "Two assistants, then the whole picture", beat 1.
- **Renderer:** `scene` (data only).
- **Claim:** Ask two assistants the same question in two fresh chats. Where they differ is where to look first. Where they agree they can still both be wrong.
- **Story:** Yara sends "who can join?" to assistants A and B. A says "students only", B says "or beginners": verdict "They differ: look here first". Then both say "4 pull requests": verdict "They agree, and both are old".
- **Facts:** Google Summer of Code is for students or open source beginners (B70); Hacktoberfest 2026 does not count pull requests (B69); two models that are both wrong give the same wrong answer 60% of the time on one leaderboard (B15). Checked 2026-10-25. The answers are invented.
- **Accessibility:** reduced motion shows the final frame, which carries the second verdict.
