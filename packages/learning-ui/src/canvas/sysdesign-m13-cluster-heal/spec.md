# Canvas spec: sysdesign-m13-cluster-heal

- **Course and chapter:** system-design, 13.3 ("Sloppy quorums, hinted handoff, read repair and Merkle trees"), beat 3, "Take nodes down, write, bring them back".
- **Renderer:** custom beat canvas on `BeatFrame`, using `SvgButton` (node cards), `SvgPillButton` (Write, Read, Wait, Reset), `BeatChoice` (Strict | Sloppy, Hints on | off), `DotGrid`, `VerdictBanner`.
- **Claim:** A sloppy write lands on stand-ins as hints and succeeds through a failure; a read of the usual nodes right after they return can miss it until the hints are handed over. Without hints it never heals.
- **Model** (`model.ts`, pure and tick based): five nodes N1 to N5, one key `x` = 6 everywhere, N = 3, W = 2, R = 2. Usual nodes N1 to N3, stand-ins N4, N5. Write: the coordinator is the first node that is up; Strict targets the usual nodes that are up; Sloppy replaces each usual node that is down with the next stand-in that is up, which keeps the value as a hint (kept apart from its own x). A target stores one tick later and acks one tick after that; the write is confirmed at 2 acks, and refused when nothing more is on the way and fewer than 2 acked. Read: instant; the newest value among the first 2 answers of the usual nodes in order; fewer than 2 live usual nodes refuses. Hints (Hints on): at every even tick a stand-in that is up offers each hint to an owner that is up (the hint is dropped, the owner stores it one tick later); a stand-in that goes down loses its hints.
- **Change from the spec:** a **Wait** chip (3 ticks, enough for one hint check and the hand-over) was added beside Write, Read and Reset. Time moves only while a message is on the way (a Write plays out by itself) or after Wait, one tick per second. Free-running time made "read at once" depend on how fast the learner taps, so the stale read could not be shown reliably.
- **Inputs:** tap a node to take it down or up; the Write, Read, Wait and Reset chips; Strict | Sloppy and Hints on | off under the picture.
- **Numbers to check** (tests): Sloppy, hints on: down N1 to N3, write 7: N4 holds a hint for N1 (7), N5 for N2 (7), "Write saved on N4 and N5 (hints)"; up all, read: 6 ("Read returned old x = 6"); wait: N1 = N2 = 7, read: 7. Hints off, wait 10 ticks: still 6, and N4 and N5 still hold the hints. Strict, N1 to N3 down: "Write refused: 0 of 2 acks". Strict, N3 down: "Write confirmed: 2 acks".
- **Picture:** node cards with `x = n` and an up or down pill (a stale usual node shows its x in amber), the coordinator outlined, hint badges under stand-ins ("for N1: 7"), answer chips under the usual nodes after a Read, message dots on curves (store accent, ack green, hand-over amber), a tick counter.
- **Reduced motion:** no dots; Write and Wait finish at once; the tick counter and badges show the result.
- **Accessibility:** each node is a button labelled "N2, up" or "N2, down"; a polite live region repeats the latest result.
- **Sources:** [S45], [S52]. The "hints are kept apart and checked on a timer" detail is flagged `source needed` in the chapter.
