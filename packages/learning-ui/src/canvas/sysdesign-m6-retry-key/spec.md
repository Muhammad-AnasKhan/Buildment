# Canvas spec: sysdesign-m6-retry-key

- **Course and chapter:** system-design, 6.4 ("Idempotency and safe retries"), beat 4 "Retry with and without a key". New slug.
- **Renderer:** custom beat canvas on `BeatFrame`, with `SvgNode` cards, `SvgChip` mode chips, `RailStrip` for the provider time, `DotGrid`, `VerdictBanner` and `BeatButton` (Replay).
- **Claim:** Without a key every timed-out retry charges again; check-then-save still double-charges when the retry lands during the charge; claiming the key first charges exactly once.
- **Model** (`models/retry-key.ts`): one purchase with challenge 5 part 4's settings. The app times out after 250 ms, waits 50 ms and retries with the same key (200 ms after a 409), at most 4 tries. The provider takes p ms for every try that reaches it, and the charge is real at (try start + p) even if the app has stopped waiting. No key: tries at 0, 300, 600, 900 and a charge per try when p > 250. Check then save: the reply is saved at p; a try that starts at or after p gets it, so one charge if p <= 300, two if p > 300. Claim first: tries at 0, 300, 500, 700 as needed; a try that finds the key pending gets 409; always one charge.
- **Inputs:** provider time on a rail, 100 to 600 ms in steps of 10, snaps at 200, 280, 450 and 600, default 450. Mode chips: No key, Check then save, Claim first (default No key). Replay outside the picture.
- **Worked values (charges, no key / check / claim):** p = 200: 1 / 1 / 1. 280: 4 / 1 / 1. 450: 4 / 2 / 1. 600: 4 / 2 / 1.
- **Picture:** cards for Huda's phone, the API (with its key store pill) and the provider; each try is a dot that flies along the lanes; a ring fills over the 250 ms wait of the try being waited on; every charge drops a coin next to the provider card, the first green and later ones red, with a running count in the provider's pill. Below, one row per try on a time strip: the top bar is the app waiting, the thin bar is the provider working, a coin marks where the charge lands, an amber dot is a 409, a green dot a saved reply; a dashed playhead sweeps the strip.
- **Verdicts:** "Answer in time: 1 charge, any mode", "No key: 4 charges, app says failed", "Saved in time: 1 charge", "Check, then save: 2 charges", "Claim first: 1 charge, always".
- **Playback:** 400 model milliseconds per second; the finished picture shows at once after any change and the run replays 1.1 seconds later, so dragging always shows the result. Under reduced motion the finished picture shows and nothing loops.
- **Accessibility:** the rail is a `role="slider"` whose value text includes the verdict; mode chips are buttons with `aria-pressed`; the SVG description gives the result in words.
- **Sources:** [S89] timeouts and retries; [S80] Stripe idempotency keys. The numbers are challenge 5's settings.
