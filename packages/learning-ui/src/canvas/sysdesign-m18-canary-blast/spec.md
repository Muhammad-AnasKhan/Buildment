# Canvas spec: sysdesign-m18-canary-blast

- **Course and chapter:** system-design, 18.6 "Deployment strategies", beat 4 ("Play: the share and the error rate").
- **Renderer:** custom beat canvas on `BeatFrame`, `TightRailSlider` with `SliderRail` (two rails), `InfoChip`, `VerdictBanner`, `useBeatClock`. `traffic-playground` is a single source-to-server lane and does not fit a split into two lanes, so the crowd is drawn here.
- **Claim:** A canary limits how many users a bad release can reach. The whole-service dashboard hides a small canary's errors, and a bigger share finds the bug sooner.
- **Model:** `model.ts`, unit tested. R = 3,000 requests a minute; stable version fails 0.2%; the canary gets share s and the bug fails e of its requests; the alert fires after F = 30 canary failures (all made up). Canary failures a minute = R x s x e; minutes to the alert = F / (R x s x e); requests exposed = R x s x minutes = F / e; dashboard rate = (1 - s) x 0.2% + s x e.
- **Check points (tested):** 5% and 20%: 30 failures a minute, 1.0 minute, 150 exposed, dashboard 1.19% (the spec rounds it to 1.2%). 5% and 2%: 3 a minute, 10 minutes, 1,500, 0.29%. 1% and 5%: 1.5 a minute, 20 minutes, 600, 0.25%. 25% and 20%: 150 a minute, 12 seconds, 150, 5.15%. The dashboard is shown with up to two decimals.
- **Inputs:** two rails drawn inside the picture. Share 1% to 50% (labelled ticks and pointer snapping at 5%, 10% and 25%); bug rate 1% to 50% (ticks and snapping at 5% and 20%). Both rails use a square-root scale so 1% to 5% has room to be dragged; arrow keys step 1%, Page keys 10%, Home and End. Outside the picture: Reset.
- **Motion:** twelve user dots stream from the request source into two lanes, a repeatable draw sends about `share` percent of them to the canary lane, and a fraction `bug` of those are red. The canary lane has a pile of 30 rings that fill red; when all 30 are filled the bell rings and the lane turns red. The pile takes 1.5 + 1.2 x log2(1 + 6 x minutes) seconds, so long waits are squeezed and still watchable, then holds full for two seconds. Under reduced motion the pile is full and the bell rings. The lane widths follow the share (the canary lane is never narrower than 96 units so its words fit). Three tiles show: alert after, requests exposed, dashboard rate.
- **Verdicts (all 38 characters or fewer; first match wins):** dashboard under 1% and bug at least 2% "Bug hidden in the average"; over 10 minutes "Slow to see: raise the share"; share 25% or more "Fast alert, but 1 in 4 users on it"; otherwise "Small share, quick alert".
- **What to notice:** requests exposed does not depend on the share (it is F / e), only on the bug rate; a bigger share shortens the wait, not the damage per alert; the dashboard stays near normal for small canaries.
- **Limits (in the fold):** made-up traffic, error rate and threshold; real alerts compare canary with stable, need a minimum sample and watch latency too.
- **Sources:** Google SRE Workbook, canarying releases: a 20% error rate on a 5% canary shows as about 1% overall [S110]. All other numbers are made up for the example.
