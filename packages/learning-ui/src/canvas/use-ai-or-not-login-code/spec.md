# Canvas spec: use-ai-or-not-login-code

- **Course and chapter:** build-with-ai (v2), 11.4 beat 3 ("When you cannot check the result").
- **Renderer:** `scene` (data only).
- **Claim:** When a mistake costs a lot and you cannot check the result, a result that "runs fine" is not enough. Get a person who can check it, or change the task so you can.

Ehab cannot tell whether passwords are stored safely, so the code may be unsafe. He asks his mentor to review it. In this made-up example the mentor finds 2 flaws. Verdict: "High stakes, no check: get a reviewer" (37 characters).
- **Numbers:** 60 members, 40 lines, 2 flaws. All made up and matching chapter 11.4.
- **Layout:** four actors on a 420 by 280 picture. Second lines are 15 characters or fewer. The verdict is under 38 characters.
