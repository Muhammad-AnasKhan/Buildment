# Canvas spec: product-m3-form-spot

- **Course and chapter:** product-sense, 3.3 "Forms people finish", beat 1 (the playable beat).
- **Renderer:** custom, on the reusable specimen spot beat (`primitives/specimen-beats.tsx`, model `models/ui-specimen.ts`). This folder holds only data. Reason: the learner must find the problems on a real form and get a reason for each tap, which a list of rules cannot do.
- **Claim:** A form fails in four common ways: a message that names no field ("Invalid input"), placeholders used as labels, an error shown only by a colour, and a small button with a vague word on it. Fixes: a visible label above each field, a hint for extra help, an error in words next to its field, and a button that says what happens [A25, A74, A23, A26, A7, A8].
- **Model:** one phone screen, Halaqa's "Start a group" form as first built. Four targets: the "Invalid input" line at the top, the box of two placeholder-only fields, the field with the value 0 and a red border and no words, and the small "Submit" button. Two fine parts: the title and the note line. The red border with no words is a field with `error: " "` (a single space), which draws the border and no message. After all four are found, a switch shows the fixed form: labels above, hints for Course and Day and time, the error "Seats must be 1 or more. Others need room to join." under the Seats field, and a full-width "Start the group" button.
- **Inputs:** tap or Tab and Enter on each part; "Start again"; after all four, the "Problems" and "Fixed form" switch.
- **What changes:** the found count ("Find the 4 problems", "Found 2 of 4", "All 4 found"), the rings, the reason line and the fixed version.
- **What the learner should notice:** each problem takes away something the person needs to finish: the name of the field, the place of the error, the words of the fix, or the knowledge of what the button does.
- **Accessibility:** every part is a focusable button with a label ("Check this part" or "<name>, found"); the reason line is a polite live region; the SVG has a title; text at least 11 units.
- **Limits (fold):** no single choice decides whether people finish a form; only a check with real people shows which problems matter. Halaqa is fictional.
- **Sources:** NN/g on placeholders [A25], labels and proximity [A64], error messages [A23]; GOV.UK Design System text input [A74]; Baymard on validation timing [A26]; WCAG 3.3.1 and 3.3.2 [A7, A8].
