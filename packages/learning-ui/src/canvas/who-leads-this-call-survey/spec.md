# Canvas spec: who-leads-this-call-survey

- **Course and chapter:** build-with-ai (v2), 11.2 beat 6 ("What developers keep for themselves").
- **Renderer:** `scene` (data only; plays on its own and loops, with Replay). It replaces a flow-chart of the same percentages, which drew a ranking as arrows.
- **Claim:** In a 2025 developer survey, most people said they do not plan to hand deployment or project planning to AI, fewer said so about writing code, and when they do not trust an AI answer, most would still ask a person.
- **Story:** five task cards fill in one by one, from the largest share to the smallest: deployment and monitoring 75.8%, project planning 69.2%, committing and reviewing code 58.7%, testing 44.1%, writing code 28.9%. The verdict is "75.3% still ask a person".
- **Numbers:** all from Stack Overflow Developer Survey 2025, AI section (A64), "all respondents". They are plans, not ability, from a self-selected community survey.
- **Layout:** five cards on a 420 by 280 picture, three in the top row and two below. Second lines are 13 characters. The verdict is 24 characters.
- **Accessibility:** the reduced-motion frame is the final one, with every share and the verdict; the beat's text and fold say the same in words.
