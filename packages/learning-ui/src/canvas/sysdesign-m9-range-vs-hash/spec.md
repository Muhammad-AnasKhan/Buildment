# Canvas spec: sysdesign-m9-range-vs-hash

- **Course and chapter:** system-design, 9.2 ("Range and hash partitioning"), beat 3, "Range or hash? Slide a week across".
- **Renderer:** custom beat canvas on `BeatFrame`. Reuses `primitives/year-strip.tsx` (the 365 day grid), `SvgNode` shard cards, `DotGrid`, `VerdictBanner` and `SliderRail`. A custom component because the picture joins a year strip, four shard cards with write bars and a field of order dots, which no renderer draws. New small primitives: `primitives/keyed-slider.tsx` (a picture slider with its own arrow and Page steps) and `primitives/beat-choice.tsx` (a pair of real buttons that acts as a switch).
- **Claim:** Range partitioning keeps a week of orders on one or two shards but sends every new write to the newest range. Hash partitioning spreads writes evenly but scatters a week over every shard.
- **Inputs:** (1) Range | Hash toggle, outside the picture, default Range. (2) The start day of a 7 day window, 1 to 359, by dragging anywhere across the picture; the rail inside it shows the position. Snaps to days 88, 179 and 271 (windows that cross a quarter border) and 120 (inside one quarter), within 2 days. Arrow keys step 1 day, Page keys 7. Default 120.
- **Model** (`model.ts`): year 2026, not a leap year; today is day 297. Range shards are quarters (days 1 to 90, 91 to 181, 182 to 273, 274 to 365). `shardsTouchedRange(start)` is the count of distinct quarters among days start to start + 6; hash always touches 4. Writes: 3,000 a second, all dated today; range puts 3,000 on shard 4, hash puts 750 on each. Limit 1,000 a second on each shard. Under hash an order's shard is `hash32("suq-order:day:i") mod 4` (seven orders a day), a fixed pseudo-random choice that always puts at least one order of any week on every shard.
- **Numbers to check:** start 120 touches 1 shard; 88, 179 and 271 touch 2; hash touches 4. Range: shard 4 at 3,000 (300% of the limit). Hash: 750 each (75%).
- **Picture:** the year strip on top with the week in blue and today ringed; four quarter brackets under it (accent when the week touches the quarter under range); four shard cards in a 2 by 2 grid, each with a status pill (writes a second, green, amber or red), a write bar against a dashed limit line (3,000 is clipped and marked "3x"; white dots run along the bar faster with more load), and a 7 by 7 field of order dots (a column per day, a row per order). Under range the week's dots fill whole columns in the shard or two it touches; under hash they scatter. Shards the week's reads must ask get an accent ring. Flipping the toggle sends the dots from the week on the strip to their cards (a transition on transform; none under reduced motion).
- **Numbers visible at once:** the verdict, three or four pills, the rail caption.
- **Verdict:** Range, one shard: "Week: 1 shard; all writes: shard 4" (warn). Range, two: "Week: 2 shards; all writes: shard 4" (warn). Hash: "Week: all 4 shards; writes even" (warn).
- **Reduced motion:** dots appear in place; no flight; the bar dots stand still.
- **Accessibility:** the picture is one `role="slider"` with value text "week starting day 120, touches 1 shard" (4 under hash). The toggle is a real button pair with `aria-pressed`. The SVG description says the split, the week and the write loads.
- **Sources:** DynamoDB hashes the partition key [S40]; per-partition write limit 1,000 a second [S40]. Suq's numbers are the course's story.
