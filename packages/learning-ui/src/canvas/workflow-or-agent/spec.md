# Workflow or agent

- **Slug:** `workflow-or-agent`
- **Course:** AI Engineering Interview, chapter 6.4 "Workflows or agents: when not to use an agent".
- **Teaching claim:** Which design wins depends on how much you weight predictability, cost and latency against handling new cases. For a request with known steps the workflow wins easily; the agent wins only when new cases matter most and the other three matter little.
- **Renderer:** factor (export `workflowOrAgent`). Two options, four criteria, three weight levels.

## Model

`total(option) = sum over criteria of weight x score`, from `renderers/factor/model.ts`. Scores are Tamer's team's example judgment on a 1 to 5 scale:

| Criterion | Workflow | Agent |
|---|---|---|
| Predictability | 5 | 2 |
| Low cost | 4 | 2 |
| Low latency | 4 | 2 |
| Handles new cases | 1 | 5 |

Weights: 1 nice to have, 2 important, 3 very important. Defaults are for "Where is my order?" messages: predictability 3, low cost 2, low latency 3, new cases 1.

Worked values: default 36 to 21 (workflow by 15). New cases 3: 38 to 31. New cases 3, predictability 1, low cost 1: 24 to 25 (agent by 1). New cases 3, the rest 1: 16 to 21. All 1: 14 to 11. All 3: 42 to 33. Highest possible total 60.

## Learning interaction

- **What this shows:** two total bars, the leader and margin, and one weight control per criterion with its score x weight line.
- **Try this:** new cases to 3 (workflow still leads 38 to 31); then predictability and low cost to 1 (agent leads 25 to 24); every weight 1 (workflow 14 to 11).
- **What changed:** generated by `describeChange` in `renderers/factor/model.ts`, for example "You raised Handles new cases from 1 (nice to have) to 3 (very important). That moved Fixed workflow +2 points, Agent +10 points. Fixed workflow still leads, now by 7 points."
- **Limits:** example scores treated as exact; risk and the router plus workflows design are left out; real choices rest on a golden set and traces.

The chapter replaces its old predict with one right before the canvas (new cases very important, the rest unchanged: who leads? Answer: the workflow) and follows the copy blocks with a transfer quiz in a new context (an incident assistant at Wadi Labs). The chapter's decision flow and table teach the claim without the canvas.
