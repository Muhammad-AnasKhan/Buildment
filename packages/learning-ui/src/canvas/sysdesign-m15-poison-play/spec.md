# Canvas spec: sysdesign-m15-poison-play

- **Course and chapter:** system-design, 15.5 ("Retries, dead letter queues and poison messages"), beat 2, "Wait in place, or park it". Playable; new slug.
- **Renderer:** custom beat canvas on `BeatFrame`: a strip of the first 22 messages as squares plus a progress bar for all 1,000, `SvgNode` cards for the consumer and the DLQ, `SnapStopsRail` for the retry limit (inside the picture, greyed while the queue is off), two counters, `DotGrid`, `VerdictBanner`. Two controls outside the picture: a toggle ("Dead letter queue", `BeatButton`) and a pair ("Always fails" or "Fails twice, then works", `BeatChoice`). `traffic-playground.tsx` was not used: its picture is a server with an inset curve, not a line of numbered messages.
- **Claim:** A message that always fails stops an ordered partition for as long as it is retried in place. A dead letter queue with a retry limit parks it so the line moves on. A limit that is too low can park a message that was only flaky.
- **Input:** queue off or on (default off); retry limit 1, 2, 3, 5, 10 (default 5; used only when the queue is on; arrows move to the next stop); message 10 always bad (default) or flaky (fails twice, then works).
- **Model** (`model.ts`, `plan` and `snapshot`): 1,000 messages in one partition; a good message 50 ms; a failed try 50 ms followed by a wait of 1 second. Off and always bad: the line stops at message 10 for ever. On and always bad: total = 9 x 0.05 + n x 1.05 + 990 x 0.05 seconds, 1 parked. Flaky and (queue off, or limit 3 or more): works on try 3, total 52.10 s. Flaky with limit 1 or 2: parked although it would have worked.
- **Numbers to check** (tested): on, always bad: limit 1, 3, 5, 10 give 51.00, 53.10, 55.20, 60.45 s, 1 parked. Flaky: limit 1 gives 51.00 s and 2 gives 52.05 s (1 parked); limit 3 or more, and queue off, give 52.10 s (0 parked). "Waiting behind it" counts the 990 messages after message 10; the spec's "991 messages wait" counts message 10 as well.
- **Picture:** squares done (green), the current one pulsing (blue, or red while failing), message 10 numbered in red, a parked message amber; a bar of all 1,000; the consumer's pill says "try 3 of 5", "waiting 1 s", "retrying" or "all done"; an arrow to the DLQ with "after 5 tries"; two counters: "waiting behind it" and "oldest has waited" (all messages are in the line from the start, so it counts model seconds; "and counting" while blocked, "for ever" at rest).
- **Verdicts:** "Blocked at message 10 for ever" (bad), "Parked after 5 tries, 55 s in all" (good; "1 try" when the limit is 1), "A good message was parked too soon" (warn), "Flaky message worked on try 3" (good).
- **Reduced motion:** the strip shows the end state (the whole line done and message 10 parked, or the line stuck at message 10).
- **Accessibility:** the counters and the consumer's status are text; the toggle and the pair are native buttons; the rail is a `role="slider"`; the SVG description spells out the plan and the total time.
- **Sources:** SQS dead-letter queues and maxReceiveCount [S85]; FIFO message groups [S84]. The 50 ms, 1 second and 1,000 messages are course numbers.
