## Next chapter — Manage products API (Chapter 11)

Chapter 11 adds **vendor product CRUD** on the server:

- `POST /products` — vendor + store ownership

- `DELETE /products/:id` — ownership  
- Vendor B cannot PATCH Vendor A product → **403**
