**Why this step matters:** Chapter 19 adds Stripe; Chapter 20 adds checkout **UI** on top of this API — you need a crisp story for split orders, snapshots, and fail-all semantics before payment and buttons.

You entered Chapter 18 with a cart and no orders. You leave with **checkout that splits by store**, **frozen receipts**, **stock that decrements safely**, and **list APIs for both sides**.

## What you built — narrative recap

**POST /checkout:** Groups cart by `storeId`; one Order per store; snapshots on every line; atomic stock decrement; fail-all on OOS; cart cleared on success.

**GET /orders/my:** Customer history from embedded snapshots — not live catalogue prices.

**GET /vendor/orders:** Vendor-scoped by store ownership; customer display fields only.

**Immutability test:** Product price PATCH does not alter past orders.

## Viva-ready answers

1. **Why split orders?** — Per-vendor fulfilment; isolation; marketplace model.
2. **Why snapshots?** — Historical truth; price edits must not rewrite receipts.
3. **Why conditional $inc?** — Prevent oversell under concurrency.
4. **Why clear cart after success?** — Prevent duplicate checkout from stale cart.
