# 24.13 — Recap and what's next

FreshMarket now behaves like a grocery app on the **inventory** axis: customers see ATP, checkout holds stock during payment, and workers clean up abandoned holds.

## What you built

| Piece | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| `StockReservation` | Time-bound holds with terminal states |
| `reserveForCheckout` | 409 before Stripe when ATP insufficient |
| Webhook confirm | Idempotent confirm + `stockQty` decrement |
| `releaseForCheckout` / expiry worker | Restore ATP without manual DB edits |
| Catalogue fields | `availableQty` for honest merchandising |

## Architecture sentence

> HTTP checkout reserves synchronously; payment webhook confirms; expiry worker releases — all transitions use conditional status updates.

## What you deliberately did not build

- Denormalized `reservedQty` on Product (aggregation ATP is enough for v1)
- Partial-line substitution flows
- Warehouse-level bins

## Next — Chapter 25: Promotions & pricing

Checkout now trusts **stock**. Chapter 25 makes checkout trust **money math**: coupon codes, vendor-funded vs platform-funded discounts, server-side recalculation, and abuse limits — never client `totalPrice`.
