## Step 1 — Create the page files

| File | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `client/src/pages/Shop.jsx` | Will hold the product grid (15.8) |
| `client/src/pages/ProductDetail.jsx` | Will show one product (15.9) |
| `client/src/api/products.js` | Functions that call Chapter 14 endpoints |

---

## Step 2 — Register routes

In `App.jsx` (or your routes file):

| Path | Component |
|---|---|
| `/shop` | '&lt;Shop />' |`
| `/shop/:productId` | '&lt;ProductDetail />' |`

These are **customer routes** — not inside `/vendor/*`.

Use the param name `productId` everywhere (route + `useParams()`).

---

## Step 3 — Add placeholder content

**Shop.jsx** for now:

- A heading: "Shop" or your marketplace name
- Text: "Products loading soon…" (you wire the fetch in 15.8)

**ProductDetail.jsx** for now:

- Read `productId` from `useParams()`
- Show it on screen so you know the route works
