```quiz
type: mcq
question: "Ashraf, a junior accountant at a small firm, wants his work to be for Allah. Every morning he delays starting for an hour, trying to make his intention perfectly pure first, and his tasks pile up. What fits this module?"
options:
  - "Set a good intention, begin the work, and renew it during the day"
  - "Keep waiting until his intention feels pure, so that the work counts"
  - "Leave intention out of work, since it belongs to prayer and fasting"
  - "Start with the tasks he enjoys, since those feel more sincere to him"
correct: 0
explanations:
  - "Right. Intention is not a test he must pass before he can act. He sets a good intention, begins, and renews it when he remembers. Actions are by intentions (Sahih al-Bukhari 1)."
  - "The hadith on intention encourages believers; it is not a gate that keeps them from working. Waiting for a perfect heart only delays the good he wants to do."
  - "Allah created us to worship Him (Quran 51:56), and with a good intention work can be part of that worship. Intention belongs in his work too."
  - "Enjoying a task does not make it more sincere. He sets the intention for all his work and does each task well."
```

```quiz
type: mcq
question: "Mona signs up for five online courses at once this summer. Each needs an hour a day, on top of her part-time job. After one week she is exhausted and behind in all five. What fits the Sunnah's teaching?"
options:
  - "Keep all five and sleep less each night until she catches up again"
  - "Keep one or two she can manage, and do them well, a little every day"
  - "Drop the courses for this summer, since the first week went badly"
  - "Wait until next summer, and then try the five courses together again"
correct: 1
explanations:
  - "No one overburdens himself without being overcome (Sahih al-Bukhari 39). Less sleep would make the overload worse, not better."
  - "Right. Take on only the deeds you can manage (Sahih Muslim 782), and the most beloved deeds are the consistent ones, even if small (Sahih al-Bukhari 6464). One or two courses, kept up, will teach her more."
  - "One week of overload is a setback, not proof that she cannot learn. The lesson is to take on less, not to stop."
  - "Trying all five again next summer repeats the same cause. A believer is not stung from the same hole twice (Sahih al-Bukhari 6133)."
```

```quiz
type: mcq
question: "Osaid, a freelance logo designer, promised a client a logo by Tuesday. On Monday he saw he would be two days late but said nothing. On Wednesday the client asks where it is. What should he do now?"
options:
  - "Say nothing more, since the delay was decreed for him in any case"
  - "Apologise, and explain that the power cuts were the real cause"
  - "Give up freelancing, since one late logo shows he is not suited to it"
  - "Tell the client honestly, apologise, give a new date, and learn"
correct: 3
explanations:
  - "Qadr does not cancel his part. His silence was a wrong to the client, and a wrong is something to put right with the person."
  - "This sounds balanced: an apology with a reason. But the cause was that he knew and said nothing, and an excuse turns an apology into a defence."
  - "One late logo is a setback, not proof that he is unfit for the work. He can learn and start again."
  - "Right. The delay is a setback, but the silence was a wrong. He puts it right with the client, asks Allah's forgiveness for the silence, and changes how he works so it does not happen twice (Sahih al-Bukhari 6133)."
```

```quiz
type: mcq
question: "Sufyan, who studies with a group in his university library, copied part of a classmate's assignment and handed it in as his own. Now he regrets it deeply, and he feels his mistake is too big for Allah to forgive. What fits the teaching?"
options:
  - "His mistake is too big to be forgiven, so he should accept that"
  - "Since Allah forgives, he does not need to do anything more about it"
  - "Ask forgiveness, not repeat it, and put right what he can"
  - "Hide it and try to forget it, so that the feeling fades in time"
correct: 2
explanations:
  - "Allah tells His servants who have wronged themselves not to despair of His mercy (Quran 39:53). His mistake is not too big to turn back from."
  - "Forgiveness is open to those who turn back, but turning back includes not persisting (Quran 3:135). A wrong to people is put right with them too."
  - "Right. Those who do wrong remember Allah, ask forgiveness and do not knowingly persist (Quran 3:135). Because the wrong touched other people, he also puts right what he can, for example by doing that work again honestly. If he is unsure what putting it right requires, he can ask a scholar he trusts."
  - "Hiding it leaves the wrong in place and the regret with nowhere to go. Turning back to Allah and putting right what he can is the way forward."
```

  - "Learn on days when she has a long free stretch, so it counts more"
  - "Keep learning a little each day, and leave the outcome with Allah"

explanations:
  - "Certainty about the future may never come. If the Hour comes with a seedling in your hand, plant it (Al-Adab al-Mufrad 479, graded sahih): effort does not wait for certainty."
  - "Whole free days are rare, and one bad day takes them. The deeds most beloved to Allah are the consistent ones, even if small (Sahih al-Bukhari 6464)."
  - "Right. She keeps planting, a little and often, and leaves the harvest with Allah. That is the seedling hadith (Al-Adab al-Mufrad 479, graded sahih) and consistency (Sahih al-Bukhari 6464) together."
  - "No one overburdens himself without being overcome (Sahih al-Bukhari 39). A month of nights would likely end in exhaustion, not readiness."
