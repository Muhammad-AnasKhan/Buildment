# 24.05 — Prime your thinking

> **[Prime]** · Chapter 24 — Inventory reservations · *Draw the timeline*

Before you add files under `server/modules/inventory/`, write a **sequence diagram on paper** (or Excalidraw) for the happy path and one sad path.

## Prompt

A customer has **2 kg tomatoes** (`stockQty: 2`) in cart. Another customer tries to buy **2 kg** while the first is on the Stripe payment screen.

List every HTTP call, webhook, and worker tick until both customers have a final outcome.


## Questions to answer in your notes

1. What HTTP status does the second customer get — 409, 400, or 200 with a partial cart?
2. If the first customer closes the tab without paying, how does ATP recover without a cron?
3. What happens if webhook confirm runs **before** your checkout handler finishes writing reservations? (Hint: idempotency keys.)

## Rubric for your sketch

| Element | Must appear |
|---|---|
| `POST /checkout` or start-payment | Reserve lines |
| Stripe client | Confirm card |
| Webhook handler | Confirm + decrement |
| Worker | Release expired `active` |
| Product GET | Shows `availableQty` |
