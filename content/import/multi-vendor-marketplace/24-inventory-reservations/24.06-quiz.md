```quiz
title: Inventory reservations
questions:
  - prompt: What is the main purpose of an active reservation before payment completes?
    choices:
      - Permanently reduce stockQty so the database stays simple
      - Reduce available-to-promise stock under concurrency without treating payment as final
      - Send a BullMQ email to the vendor

  - prompt: When should stockQty decrement for a sold unit?
    choices:
      - When the item is added to cart
      - When the customer opens the product page
      - When payment succeeds and the reservation is confirmed
      - When the reservation expires
    correct: 2
  - prompt: Two checkouts race for the last unit. What should the loser receive?
    choices:
      - 200 with an unpaid order
      - 409 or equivalent insufficient-stock response before payment
      - 500 internal error

    correct: 1
  - prompt: Why must confirmReservation be idempotent?
    choices:

      - MongoDB forbids updates

      - JWT refresh tokens expire
    correct: 0
  - prompt: An active reservation expires in the worker. What happens to stockQty?
    choices:
      - Increases by the held quantity
      - Unchanged; ATP rises because activeHeld drops
      - Decreases because the sale failed
      - Deleted from the product document
    correct: 1
```
