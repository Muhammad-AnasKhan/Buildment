# 24.13 — Recap and what's next

> **[Wrap]** · Chapter 24 — Inventory reservations

FreshMarket now behaves like a grocery app on the **inventory** axis: customers see ATP, checkout holds stock during payment, and workers clean up abandoned holds.

## What you built

| Piece | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| `StockReservation` | Time-bound holds with terminal states |
| Product `reservedQty` | Atomic contention point for concurrent claims |
| `reserveForCheckout` | Guarded claim + reservation in one transaction |
| Webhook confirm | Idempotent confirm; decrement `stockQty` and `reservedQty` together |
| `releaseForCheckout` / expiry worker | Decrement `reservedQty` without changing on-hand stock |
| Catalogue fields | `availableQty = stockQty - reservedQty` for honest merchandising |

## Architecture sentence

> HTTP checkout claims `reservedQty` synchronously; payment webhook confirms; expiry worker releases—every terminal transition and counter update commits atomically.

## What you deliberately did not build

- Multi-warehouse or batch-level reservation counters
- Partial-line substitution flows
- Warehouse-level bins

## Next — Chapter 25: Promotions & pricing

Checkout now trusts **stock**. Chapter 25 makes checkout trust **money math**: coupon codes, vendor-funded vs platform-funded discounts, server-side recalculation, and abuse limits — never client `totalPrice`.
