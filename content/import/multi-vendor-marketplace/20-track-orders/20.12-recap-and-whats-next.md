## The consistency story

One paid checkout now produces store-scoped orders that both actors recognize:

```text
Stripe-verified payment
  → authoritative split orders with immutable line snapshots
  → customer confirmation and history
  → each vendor's isolated fulfilment queue
  → validated status transition
  → customer refresh shows the same saved status
```

The UI never recalculates paid prices from the catalogue, treats `paymentStatus` as a fulfilment step, or assumes a status update succeeded. Shared components keep formatting consistent; server-scoped routes keep access consistent.

You also covered the uncomfortable paths: declined payment, payment-success/order-response uncertainty, confirmation refresh, empty histories, fetch failures, cross-vendor access, stale two-tab updates, and keyboard/status announcements.
