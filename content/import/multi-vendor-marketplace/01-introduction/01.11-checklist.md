# 1.11 — Checklist and learning log

> **[Gate]** · Chapter 01 — Introduction

This is the **gate** for Chapter 1. You do not need a repository yet — you need clarity on what you are building and how you will work through the course.

## Chapter 1 checklist

- [ ] **FreshMarket story** — you can explain vendor vs customer in one paragraph
- [ ] **In scope vs out of scope** — you know what ships in four weeks vs what waits
- [ ] **Working rhythm** — you have a weekly plan (time blocks, when you code vs read)
- [ ] **Course map** — you know Chapter 2 starts the repo and Chapter 5 implements models
- [ ] **Prerequisites** — Node, Git, and MongoDB tooling are installed or scheduled
- [ ] **Engineering habits** — you will commit small steps and ask for help when stuck

## Learning log

Answer **in your own words** (you can use the microphone on each field):

1. **Why build a multi-vendor grocery marketplace** instead of a single-store app? Tie your answer to FreshMarket.

2. **Your role this month:** Are you leaning vendor-path, customer-path, or full-stack? How will that change what you pay attention to in each module?

3. **Risk you see in week 3** — scope creep, auth confusion, or data model drift? Pick one and how you will avoid it.

4. **When you will work on this course** each week — be specific (days, hours).

5. **Repo plan:** When Chapter 2 starts, will you use a new GitHub repo or an existing one? Name the repo you intend to use (you can paste the link later in Chapter 2).
