# 25.13 — Recap and what's next

> **[Wrap]** · Chapter 25 — Promotions & pricing

FreshMarket now handles two production problems that separate demos from businesses: **honest inventory** (Ch 24) and **honest pricing** (Ch 25).

## What you built

- **Coupon** + **CouponRedemption** models with caps and windows
- **validateCoupon** pipeline and cart apply/remove routes
- **computeCartPricing** — single authority for UI and Stripe
- Order snapshots with `discountCents` / `couponCode`
- Rate limiting on coupon attempts

## Interview story

> “We never trust client totals. The server recomputes on apply and at checkout, allocates discounts across multi-vendor lines, and records redemptions append-only for audits.”
