## Learning log

Answer **in your own words**:

1. **Why MongoDB for your marketplace?** Give two reasons tied to this project.

2. **Steelman SQL:** What is the strongest argument for PostgreSQL for this same product?

3. **Embed vs reference:** Walk through checkout — what is embedded, what is referenced, what gets copied?

4. **Denormalisation:** Why is copying `vendorName` onto every product dangerous but copying `priceAtPurchase` correct?

5. **Actor isolation:** How does your ERD ensure Vendor A cannot update Vendor B's product?
