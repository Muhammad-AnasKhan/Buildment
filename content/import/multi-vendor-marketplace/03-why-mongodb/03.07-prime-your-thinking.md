## What you'll do

Before you create `docs/erd-draft.md`, read MongoDB's official data modelling guide. This aligns your design with the vocabulary engineers and interviewers recognize.

## Why this matters

Course prose and official docs use slightly different words for the same ideas. Reading MongoDB's modelling introduction **once** means your ERD and interview answers use vocabulary employers recognize — "embedding for contained data," "reference when entity has independent lifecycle."

<Callout type="info">
You're not learning MongoDB from zero here. You're aligning your sketch with how the manual describes relationships.
</Callout>
