## Learning log

1. **Missing steps:** When you wrote or read steps in this module, which step was easiest to forget because it felt obvious? Sami's mother forgot the water. What is your "water"?
2. **Patterns:** Think of a pattern you see in a normal week, such as bus times or market prices, or use Basel's phone cases from chapter 2.3. How sure are you that it will continue, and what could break it?
3. **Faster ways:** Sorting by small piles was faster than searching the whole pile each time. Where else could splitting a big job into small parts save you work?
4. **Flowcharts:** Which decision do you make often that could become a small flowchart? What would its first yes or no question be?

## Your written steps

Choose a task you know well from daily life, study or work: cooking a simple dish, setting up a phone for a relative, or sending a job application. If you prefer, use the provided task: prepare a room for a study group of six people. A simple numbered list is fine, and you do not need perfect English. A mentor will read your steps and imagine following them exactly as written. If you can, give your steps to someone else first and watch them follow along without your help. Every question they ask shows a step to add or make clearer. Your steps do not need to be long: eight clear steps are better than twenty vague ones.
