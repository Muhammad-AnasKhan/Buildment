```quiz
question: "A phone credit offer gives 5 shekels of free credit if you top up 20 shekels or more and you have had your number for at least a year. Who gets the free credit?"
options:
  - "Huda tops up 15 shekels and has had her number for three years"
  - "Omar tops up 50 shekels and got his number six months ago"
  - "Lina tops up 10 shekels and got her number last week"
  - "Yousef tops up 30 shekels and got his number two years ago"
correct: 3
explanations:
  - "Huda has had her number long enough, but 15 shekels is less than 20. With 'and', both parts must be true."
  - "Omar tops up enough, but six months is less than a year. One false part makes the whole condition false."
  - "Lina fails both parts: 10 shekels is less than 20, and her number is new."
  - "Right. 30 shekels is 20 or more, and two years is at least a year. Both parts are true."
```

```quiz
type: mcq
```
```quiz
question: "A repair shop has a rule it always keeps: 'If a laptop is in the Ready box, it has been tested.' Amal's laptop has been tested. What can you say for sure?"
options:
  - "Her laptop is in the Ready box"
  - "Her laptop is not in the Ready box"
  - "The rule does not say where it is"
  - "Her laptop was never broken"
correct: 2
explanations:
  - "This reads the rule backwards. A tested laptop could also be waiting for a second repair, or already with its owner."
  - "This goes too far the other way. Her laptop may be in the Ready box. The rule cannot tell us."
  - "Right. The then-part (tested) is true, and there are other places a tested laptop could be. Nothing follows for sure."
  - "Being tested says nothing about whether the laptop was broken. This is not in the rule at all."
```

```quiz
  - "Nothing: the rule is about finishing all ten"
  - "He will not get a certificate this time"
  - "He gets one, because seven is most of ten"
  - "He gets a smaller certificate for seven lessons"
correct: 0
explanations:
  - "Right. The if-part is false for Sami, and the rule says nothing about people who finish fewer lessons. There may be another rule we do not know."
  - "This sounds likely, but it does not follow. When the if-part is false, the rule says nothing. The course may have another way to earn one."
  - "The rule asks for all ten lessons. Most of them is not all of them, so the rule does not give him one."
  - "The rule does not mention a smaller certificate. This adds something the rule never said."
```

```quiz
  - "Back to Being fixed, for a second check"
  - "On to Collected, when he picks it up"
  - "Back to Received, to start again"
  - "No move, because Ready is an end state"

explanations:
  - "No arrow goes from Ready back to Being fixed. The rules allow only one move out of Ready."
  - "Right. The only move out of Ready is to Collected."
  - "No rule moves a job back to Received. Moves the rules do not allow change nothing."
  - "Ready is not an end state, because one move leaves it: to Collected."
```
