```quiz
type: mcq
question: "Five friends spend these amounts on phone credit in a month: 20, 25, 20, 25 and 160 shekels. Basel says: 'The average is 50 shekels, so 50 is a normal amount for us.' What is the best reply?"
options:
  - "One big number pulls the average up. The middle value is 25"
  - "The average is right, so 50 shekels is a normal amount for us"
  - "The sum is wrong. The average of these amounts is 35 shekels"
  - "The middle value is 20, because 20 appears in the list twice"
correct: 0
explanations:
  - "Right. In order: 20, 20, 25, 25, 160. The middle is 25. The one large amount, 160, pulls the average up to 50, which is at least double what each of the other four spends."
  - "The sum is right: 250 / 5 = 50. But four of the five friends spend 25 or less, so 50 is not a normal amount here."
  - "Check the sum: 20 + 25 + 20 + 25 + 160 = 250, and 250 / 5 = 50. The average is 50, not 35."
  - "The middle value is the one in the middle when the numbers are in order: 20, 20, 25, 25, 160. That is 25, not the number that appears most."
```

```quiz
type: mcq
question: "Rania is paid 400 dollars for a job on a freelance website that keeps 10 percent of every payment. She spent 20 hours on the job, counting messages and changes. What is her real pay per hour?"
options:
  - "20 dollars an hour"
  - "18 dollars an hour"
  - "16 dollars an hour"
  - "2 dollars an hour"
correct: 1
explanations:
  - "This is 400 / 20, which forgets the fee. Rania does not receive the full 400 dollars."
  - "Right. 10 percent of 400 is 40, so she receives 400 - 40 = 360 dollars. 360 / 20 hours = 18 dollars an hour."
  - "This takes the fee off twice: 400 - 80 = 320, and 320 / 20 = 16. The fee is 40 dollars, taken once."
  - "This is the fee per hour: 40 / 20 = 2. It is what the website keeps, not what Rania receives."
```

  - "Her number is more exact than the number Sami gave"
  - "Her answer is longer, so she must have worked harder"
  - "She gave a range, so it is sure to include the truth"
  - "Others can see and test each guess she used"
correct: 3
explanations:
  - "Both numbers are the same: about 300. Neither is more exact than the other."
  - "Length is not the point. What matters is what the extra words show: the guesses behind the number."
  - "Maryam gave one number, not a low and a high one. And a range is never sure to include the truth."
  - "Right. 10 stalls x 30 people = 300. Anyone can check the number of stalls or watch one stall for an hour, then improve her estimate."
