```quiz
type: mcq
question: "A message says: 'Either you buy the most expensive revision course, or you will fail your exams.' What is missing from this argument?"
options:
  - "The other ways to prepare for the exams"
  - "The name of an expert who likes the course"
  - "A list of everyone who bought the course"
  - "A warning about what happens if you fail"
correct: 0
explanations:
  - "Right. This is the trap 'only two choices'. You could also study with a group, use free materials or practice old exam papers."
  - "An expert's name alone would not fix the argument. The problem is that it hides all the other options."
  - "Many buyers would only add 'everyone does it'. It still would not show this is the only way to pass."
  - "The message already uses fear of failing. More fear adds no reason."
```

```quiz
type: mcq
question: "Nour gets a message: 'Congratulations, you got the online tutor job at 15 dollars an hour. Please pay a 30 dollar training fee before your first lesson.' Which warning sign does this message show?"
options:
  - "It asks for your password"
  - "It pushes you to act today"
  - "The pay is far too high for the work"
  - "You must pay before you get paid"
correct: 3
explanations:
  - "The message does not ask for a password or any login details."
  - "The message gives no deadline. It does not say 'today' or 'tonight'."
  - "15 dollars an hour for tutoring is not a wild promise. The problem is somewhere else in the message."
  - "Right. A fee before the first paid lesson is the sign 'pay to get paid'. Nour should find the company's official contact herself and check."
```

```quiz
type: mcq
question: "Omar pastes a forwarded message into an AI tool: 'Doctors say drinking ginger tea every morning stops all colds.' He asks if it is true. The AI tool answers: 'Yes, this is reliable, because doctors say it.' What is the main problem with the AI's answer?"
options:
  - "It should have said how much ginger tea to drink"
  - "It trusts 'doctors say' with no name or evidence"
  - "It is right, because doctors are experts on colds"
  - "It should have checked how many people shared it"
correct: 1
explanations:
  - "An amount would not help if the claim itself is not shown. First, the claim needs evidence."
  - "Right. 'Doctors say' names no doctor and shows no study. Using it as the only reason is the trap 'an expert said so', and the AI tool repeated it."
  - "Real doctors with evidence would be a good reason. But no doctor is named and no evidence is given, so there is nothing to check."
  - "Many shares would only show the message is popular. Popular is not the same as true."
```

```quiz
type: mcq
question: "Yousef says: 'I will not use the new bus route, because it was late twice last week.' Which reply starts with a fair summary of his view?"
options:
  - "So you think every bus in this city is always late?"
  - "You just do not like trying anything new, do you?"
  - "It was late twice, so you do not trust it yet"
  - "Everyone else uses the new route with no problems"
correct: 2
explanations:
  - "This twists his words. Yousef talked about one route in one week, not every bus."
  - "This attacks Yousef instead of his reason. He gave a clear reason, and this reply ignores it."
  - "Right. Yousef would agree with this. It says his view and his reason in other words, so he will feel heard."
  - "This uses 'everyone does it' and skips his view. It is not a summary of what Yousef said."
```

```quiz
type: mcq
question: "Maryam writes: 'Our group should start revising two weeks before the exam. Last term we started one week before, and four of us ran out of time.' Which part is her evidence?"
options:
  - "Start revising two weeks early"
  - "Four ran out of time last term"
  - "Revising early is always better"
  - "The exam this term will be harder"
correct: 1
explanations:
  - "This is her claim: what she wants the group to do."
  - "Right. It is something that happened, and the group could check it. It supports her claim."
  - "Maryam does not say this. It is a general belief, not something she shows."
  - "Maryam says nothing about this term's exam. It is not part of her argument."
```
