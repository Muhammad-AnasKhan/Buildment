## Module 7 checklist

- [ ] I can explain in plain words why an AI chat tool can sound sure and still be wrong

## Learning log

1. **Sure and wrong:** Think of a time a person, a message or a tool told you something in a sure voice, and it turned out wrong. What made it sound true? If you prefer, use Khaled's essay from chapter 7.1.
2. **Your weakest part:** Of the five parts of a clear instruction, which one do you leave out most often? Why do you think that is?
3. **Your first check:** When an AI answer has a number in it, which check will you try first, and why that one?
4. **Your line:** Write one sentence that says when you will not use an AI tool. Make it specific to your study or work.

## Rewrite a vague instruction

Pick one vague instruction you have given, or received, at study, at work or at home. "Help me with my CV" or "Make a plan for the meeting" are typical. Then rewrite it in the five parts. Write it twice: once for a person who knows you, and once for an AI tool that knows nothing about you. Notice what you have to add for the tool.

A simple list is fine. You do not need perfect English. A mentor will read your answer and reply. If you prefer not to use your own life, use this one: Rania asks her cousin, and then an AI tool, to "make a plan for our study group". The group has six people, meets twice a week for three weeks, and is preparing for an English exam.
