```quiz
type: mcq
question: "Which of these questions is an AI chat tool most likely to get wrong, even in a calm, sure voice?"
options:
  - "Explain in simple words what a family budget is"
  - "Suggest five names for a small sewing shop"
  - "Say the opening hours of a shop in your street"
  - "Make a short thank-you message sound warmer"
correct: 2
explanations:
  - "Explaining a common idea is a job AI tools usually do well. You can also judge the explanation yourself."
  - "Ideas are a good job for an AI tool. You choose which names to keep."
  - "Right. This is an exact, local fact. The tool may never have seen it, so it may write hours that only sound normal."
  - "Rewording is a good job for an AI tool. You can read the result and see if the meaning stayed the same."
```

```quiz
type: mcq
question: "Tamer asks an AI chat tool how much he will earn. It says: 'You will work 30 hours at 12 dollars an hour. After the website keeps 10 percent, you will get 450 dollars.' What is wrong with this answer?"
options:
  - "Nothing, 450 dollars is right after the fee"
  - "30 x 12 is only 360, so 450 is far too high"
  - "The website fee should be 20 percent here"
  - "It should add the fee on top, not take it off"
correct: 1
explanations:
  - "Check the rough numbers first. 30 hours at 12 dollars is 360 dollars, so he cannot get 450 after a fee."
  - "Right. 30 x 12 is 360 dollars before the fee. After the website keeps 10 percent (36 dollars), he gets 324 dollars, not 450."
  - "The question says the website keeps 10 percent. The problem is not the fee, it is the sum."
  - "A fee the website keeps is taken off what he earns, not added to it. And even 360 plus a fee is not 450."
```

```quiz

  - "I bake at home. Delivery starts this Friday"
  - "Make it friendly, warm and under 50 words"
  - "First the news, then the price, then thanks"
  - "It is good if it names Friday and the price"
correct: 0
explanations:
  - "Right. Context is what the tool needs to know and cannot guess: a home bakery, and delivery starting on Friday."
  - "This is the format part: the tone and the length of the answer."
  - "This is the steps part: the order of the post."
  - "This is the how-to-check part: what makes the post good."
```

```quiz
type: mcq
question: "An AI chat tool tells Omar: 'Studies show that students who study after midnight remember 40 percent more.' What should Omar check first?"
options:
  - "Whether 40 percent is a big enough change"
  - "Whether he can stay awake after midnight"
  - "Whether the tool is really sure about it"
  - "Which study it is, so he can read it"
correct: 3
explanations:
  - "The size of the number does not matter yet. First he needs to know if the claim is real at all."
  - "This is about his habits, not about whether the claim is true."
  - "Asking the same tool again is not a real check. It can say 'yes' in the same sure voice."
  - "Right. 'Studies show' is a name, not a source. Until Omar finds the study and reads it, the claim is unchecked."
```

```quiz

  - "Say nothing, since he built the layout himself"
  - "Say nothing, since the client allowed AI ideas"
  - "Tell the client he used AI for layout ideas"
  - "Start again without AI, to be completely safe"
correct: 2
explanations:
  - "Building it himself is good, but the client asked to be told about any AI use for ideas."
  - "The client allowed it on one condition: tell me when you do. Saying nothing breaks that condition."
  - "Right. He followed the rule by using AI only for ideas, and he follows the second half by saying so."
  - "There is no need to start again. His use was allowed. He only needs to tell the client."
```
