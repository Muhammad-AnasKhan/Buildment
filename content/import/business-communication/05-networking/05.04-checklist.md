## Module 5 checklist

- [ ] My headline names my role, what I do and for whom

## Learning log

1. **Your person:** Go back to the person you named in chapter 5.1. What small thing did you give them first, or what could you give them this week?
2. **Your headline:** What did your headline say before this module, and what does it say now? What changed?
3. **Your request:** Why is the request in your message the right size for where your relationship is now?
4. **Silence:** If your message gets no reply, what will you do, and when?
