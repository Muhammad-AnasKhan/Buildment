This is the final gate of the course. Tick each box only when it is true. Your answers save as you type.

## Module 7 checklist

- [ ] I submitted my communication pack in chapter 7.2, with all four pieces
- [ ] My kickoff email, status update and bad-news message use the same names, dates and scope
- [ ] My bad-news message has facts, impact, options and a recommendation
- [ ] I said my interview answer aloud at least once, in about two minutes or less
- [ ] I saved my four pieces somewhere I can find them for my next real project

## Learning log

1. **Hardest piece:** Which of the four pieces was hardest to write, and why? What will you do differently when you write one for a real client?
2. **Two habits to keep:** Choose two habits from this course, for example a request in the first two lines of every email, or a done, next and blockers update every week. When will you use each one in the next month?
3. **One habit to drop:** Which old habit will you stop? For example, waiting until the deadline to share a delay, or ending a message with no clear next step.
4. **Your next real message:** Name one real message you need to write in the next two weeks: a client email, an update, an outreach note or an interview answer. Which checklist from chapter 7.2 will you use for it?
5. **Advice to a new fellow:** If a friend starts this course tomorrow, what is one thing you would tell them about communicating with international teams and clients?
