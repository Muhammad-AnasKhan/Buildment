This is the gate for module 1. Tick each box only when it is true. Your answers save as you type.

## Module 1 checklist

- [ ] I can turn a bare command, such as "Send me the file.", into a polite question
- [ ] I can name four polite tools in English: questions, softeners, a reason and thanks
- [ ] I can explain scope, deliverable, ETA, blocker, follow up, stakeholder, EOD, ASAP, FYI and sync in my own words
- [ ] I write deadlines for people in other time zones as a time and a zone, not only "EOD"
- [ ] I answered the questions in chapters 1.2 and 1.3, and the quiz in 1.4
- [ ] I submitted the rewrite of my own message below

## Rewrite one of your own messages

Go back to the message you shared in chapter 1.1. If you no longer have it, use another real message you sent or plan to send. Remove any private details, such as names of clients, prices or passwords.

## Learning log

1. **Two languages:** Which polite habits from your own language do you want to keep in English messages, such as a warm greeting or asking about the person?
2. **Your risky phrases:** Which phrase from the table in chapter 1.2 do you think you use most often? What will you write instead?
3. **New terms:** Which term from chapter 1.3 was new to you? Where have you seen it, or where do you expect to see it?
4. **Time zones:** Who do you work with, or hope to work with, in another time zone? How will you write deadlines so they are clear to them?
