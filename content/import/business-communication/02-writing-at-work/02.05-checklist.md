## Learning log

1. **Your slow message:** Go back to the message you described in chapter 2.1. Rewrite its first line so the reader knows at once what you need.
2. **Your inbox:** Look at the last five emails you sent. How many had a subject that said what the email was about? How many put the request in the first two lines?
3. **Your blockers:** Think of a time you were stuck on a task and waited before asking for help. How long did you wait, and what stopped you from asking earlier?
4. **Your hours:** Which hours are you usually online for work or study? Who needs to know them, and where will you write them down for others?

## Your standup update and email
