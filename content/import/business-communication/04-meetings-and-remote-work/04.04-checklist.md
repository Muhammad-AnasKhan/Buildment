## Practice meeting (if you have no real one)

If you do not have a real meeting this week, run a short practice meeting with your cohort, your study group or your mentor.

- **Time:** 15 minutes.
- **Roles:** one facilitator, one note-taker, and two or three team members.
- **Scenario:** your group is building a one-page website for a local NGO's volunteer sign-up. You need to split the work.
- **Goal:** agree who does the text, the design and the sign-up form, and the date for each.
- **Facilitator:** send the agenda before you start, keep to time, and read the action items aloud at the end.
- **Note-taker:** write decisions, action items with owners and dates, and open questions.

After the meeting, the group answers two questions together: Did we reach the goal? Did every task get one owner and a date?
