## Learning log

1. **Your role:** Go back to the role you named in chapter 6.1. After this module, what do you think the interviewer most wants to learn about you? Did your view change?
2. **Timing:** Say your STAR answer aloud with a timer. How long did it take? Which part was the longest, and was it the action?
3. **Follow-up:** What follow-up question might an interviewer ask after your story? Write a short, honest answer to it.
4. **Your worry:** Look at the worry you wrote in chapter 6.1. What is one thing you will do to prepare for it before your next interview?

## Your interview answer

Choose one behavioral question, for example "Tell me about a time you solved a difficult problem" or "Tell me about a time you made a mistake." Answer it with a true story from your story bank, using STAR. A mentor will review it. You can build on this answer in the capstone.
