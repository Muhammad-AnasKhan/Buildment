## Rewrite one of your own messages

Go back to the message you described in chapter 1.1. If you no longer have it, use another real message you sent in the last month. Remove any private details, such as names of clients, prices or passwords.

```openquestion
prompt: "Paste your original message (or a short summary of it). Then write your new version with the point in the first sentence and, if it has several supporting points, two to four clear groups. End with one sentence on what you changed and why."
required: true
minWords: 60
allowSpeechInput: true
modelAnswer: "Original: 'Hi Omar, I worked on the API this week. The login part is done and I wrote tests for it. I had some problems with the payment provider documentation, it was not clear. I also need to know if we support cards only or wallets too, because it changes the design. Also next week I am starting the notifications.' New version: 'Hi Omar, do we support cards only, or wallets too? I need to know by Wednesday, because it changes the payment design. Done: login is finished and tested. Next week: payments, then notifications.' What I changed: I moved my question from the middle to the first line and added a deadline. I grouped the rest into Done and Next week, and cut the part about the unclear documentation, because Omar did not need it."
```

## Learning log

1. **Your habit:** When you write a message, where does your main point usually end up: at the start, in the middle or at the end? Why do you think that happens?
2. **The rewrite:** What was the hardest part of rewriting your own message: finding the point, choosing groups, or cutting details?
3. **The reader:** Think of one person you write to often, such as a team lead, client or teacher. What do they most need to see in your first line?
4. **Next time:** What is one small habit you will use before sending your next important message?
