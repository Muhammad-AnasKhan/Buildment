This is the gate for module 2. First prepare your explanation, then tick each box only when it is true. Your answers save as you type.

Choose one technical idea from your own work, study or project. Then:

1. Name one real listener who does not work in tech, such as a relative, a friend or a client.
2. Write down what they already know and what they need from you.
3. Write your explanation with the four steps from chapter 2.4.
4. Try it on that person, or someone like them. A phone call or a voice message is fine. Change the parts where they got lost.
5. Submit the final version below, written or recorded.

## Module 2 checklist

- [ ] I can explain the curse of knowledge in my own words, with an example
- [ ] Before writing, I noted what my listener knows and what they need from me
- [ ] My explanation uses at least one concrete example or comparison, and I checked where the comparison stops
- [ ] I tried my explanation on one person outside tech and changed at least one part
- [ ] I answered the questions in chapters 2.2, 2.3 and 2.4

## Learning log

1. **Your listener:** Who did you choose? What did you decide they already knew, and what did they need?
2. **Where they got lost:** When you tried your explanation, where did the listener stop following you? What did you change?
3. **Jargon check:** Which technical words did you remove or explain? Which one was hardest to replace?
4. **Back to chapter 2.1:** Look at the story you wrote about getting lost, or losing someone. Which tool from this module would have helped most?
