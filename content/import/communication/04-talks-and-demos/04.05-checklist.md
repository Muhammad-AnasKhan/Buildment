## Module 4 checklist

- [ ] I can plan a five-minute talk with a point, three supporting parts and a clear ending
- [ ] I can name the three parts of a demo: the problem, the result and one key decision
- [ ] I can list three demo backups for weak internet: a recorded demo, screenshots and a local copy
- [ ] I can answer "I do not know" in three parts: what I know, what I will do, and when
- [ ] I answered the questions in chapters 4.1 to 4.4
- [ ] I submitted my talk outline below

## Learning log

1. **Your project:** Go back to the project you named in chapter 4.1. Is your one main point still the same, or did it change while you wrote the outline? Why?
2. **Timing:** Say your outline out loud with a timer. How long did it take? What did you cut, or what would you cut?
3. **What could break:** If you demoed this project on a call tomorrow, what is most likely to go wrong? Which backup would you prepare first?
4. **The hard question:** What is the one question you would least like to be asked about this project? Write a short, honest answer to it.

## Your talk outline

Plan a five-minute talk or demo about your own project, from work, study, freelancing or a personal project. Write it as an outline, not a full script: short lines are fine. You will build on this outline in the capstone, so make it real. A mentor will review it.

Part 1, the problem (1 minute): power and internet cuts meant students lost their plans and missed group sessions. Part 2, what I built (1.5 minutes): a demo of the happy path. Add a study session, lose the connection, and the plan is still there. Then it syncs when the internet is back. Part 3, one key decision (1 minute): I stored data on the phone first and synced later, instead of saving online only. It was harder to build, but it works with weak internet. Ending (30 seconds): an offline-first planner that real students use. I would be glad to show how the sync works in the technical interview. Backup plan: a screen recording of the demo on my laptop, screenshots of each step, and the app running locally. I will send the recording link before the call and keep mobile data ready. Expected question: what happens if two devices change the same plan? Short answer: the newest change wins for now. I know this can lose data, and fixing it is my next task."
