This is the final gate of the course. Tick each box only when it is true. Your answers save as you type.

## Module 6 checklist

- [ ] I submitted my talk in chapter 6.3, as a recording link or a full written script
- [ ] My talk states the main point in the first 30 seconds and again at the end
- [ ] My talk has an opening, three parts and an ending
- [ ] I practiced it out loud at least once with a timer
- [ ] I can name one tool from each of the five modules in my own words

## Learning log

1. **Most useful tool:** Which tool from this course will you use most in the next month? Describe the message, call or meeting where you expect to use it.
2. **Your practice run:** What surprised you when you practiced out loud or listened to your recording? What did you change because of it?
3. **Listening:** Think of a recent conversation where you planned your reply instead of listening. What will you do differently next time?
4. **Your next talk:** What is the next talk, demo or update you will give? Who is the listener, what is your main point, and when is it?
5. **Advice to a new fellow:** If a friend starts this course tomorrow, what is one thing you would tell them?
