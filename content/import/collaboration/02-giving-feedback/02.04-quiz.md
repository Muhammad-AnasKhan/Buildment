Six short work scenarios about giving feedback. Each one has one best answer. Read the explanation after every question, even when you get it right: the reasons matter more than the score.

```quiz
type: mcq
question: "Amira's teammate Elias wrote a setup guide for the project. Yesterday two new interns used it to set up the project without asking Amira a single question. Which message gives Elias the most useful praise?"
options:
  - "Elias, great job on the setup guide! You are honestly one of the best people on this team"
  - "Elias, yesterday two interns set up the project from your guide with no questions to me"
  - "Elias, thanks for the guide. Everyone on the team keeps saying how good and helpful it is"
  - "Elias, the guide is good. Next time maybe add some screenshots so it looks more complete"
correct: 1
explanations:
  - "This is warm, but it does not say what Elias did well, so he does not know what to repeat."
  - "Right. It names the moment, what his guide made possible, and the effect on Amira. Elias knows exactly what worked."
  - "Second-hand praise is vague. Elias still does not know which part of the guide helped people."
  - "This turns praise into a new task. The idea may be fine, but the useful praise is lost behind it."
```

```quiz
type: mcq
question: "For a month, Sami's teammate Jana has joined the daily team call about 15 minutes late. The call is short, so she misses most of the updates and asks for them again in chat. Sami has said nothing so far. What should he do?"
options:
  - "Post a reminder in the team channel that everyone must join the daily call on time"
  - "Wait for Jana's performance review in two months, when feedback is usually given"
  - "Ask the team lead to talk to Jana, so that Sami does not have to say anything himself"
  - "Message Jana privately, name the late joins and their effect, and ask what is going on"
correct: 3
explanations:
  - "A general message to the whole team avoids the real conversation. Jana may not even think it is about her."
  - "Waiting two more months means more missed updates, and the feedback will come too late to be useful."
  - "Going to the lead first, before Jana has heard anything, can feel like going behind her back: acting about her without telling her first."
  - "Right. Silence was already sending a message. A private, specific message with a question lets Jana explain and change."
```

```quiz
type: mcq
question: "Nuha is giving feedback to a teammate about a client. Which sentence describes behavior, in the SBI sense?"
options:
  - "You replied to the client's last two emails four days after they arrived"
  - "You do not seem to take this client as seriously as the rest of us do"
  - "You are a bit too slow with emails, and the client has started to notice it"
  - "You need to be more responsive and professional with all of our clients"
correct: 0
explanations:
  - "Right. This is something a camera could record: two emails, four days. The teammate can check it and act on it."
  - "This is a guess about the teammate's attitude. They will defend themselves instead of hearing the point."
  - "'Too slow' is a judgment without detail. How slow, and for which emails? The teammate has to guess."
  - "This is a general instruction, not a description of anything the teammate did."
```

```quiz
type: mcq
question: "Noor and Felix are freelancers working together for one client. Felix sent his part of the work on Thursday, not Tuesday as agreed. Noor is writing feedback using SBI. Which impact statement is best?"
options:
  - "It made it look like you do not really care about this client or this project"
  - "I think you were probably busy with another client who pays you a bit better"
  - "Because your part came on Thursday, we missed the client's Wednesday review"
  - "It caused big problems for everyone involved, and it was honestly a bad week"
correct: 2
explanations:
  - "This guesses at Felix's feelings. Noor does not know them, and the guess will start an argument."
  - "This guesses at his reason. Felix may have had a power cut or been ill, and the guess may be wrong."
  - "Right. It states a real, checkable effect of the late work, without guessing why it happened."
  - "This is too vague. Felix cannot tell what the problems were or how big they were."
```

```quiz
type: mcq
question: "Ahmad reviewed a teammate's README for an open-source project. He found two real problems, a wrong API address and a missing setup step, and five small wording ideas. Which summary should he write at the top of his review?"
options:
  - "Lots of comments below. Please work through all of them carefully before we can merge this one"
  - "Two must-change points: the wrong API address and a missing setup step. Five optional ideas below"
  - "Looks good to me overall! I left a few small thoughts below, but nothing too serious, really"
  - "This README needs quite a lot of work before it is ready. Please see my comments and fix all of them"
correct: 1
explanations:
  - "This treats all seven comments the same. The author may fix the wording ideas first and miss the real problems."
  - "Right. The author sees at once what must change and that the rest is optional."
  - "This hides two real problems. Readers would follow a wrong address and a broken setup."
  - "This makes optional ideas sound required, and it does not say which points really matter."
```

```quiz
type: mcq
question: "Ines leads a remote team from Lisbon. In a demo for a client, Bilal, a developer in Gaza, showed a screen with another client's private data on it. Ines needs to give him feedback. What should she do?"
options:
  - "Message Bilal privately today, and offer a short call at a time that suits both"
  - "Post the feedback in the shared project channel, so that the whole team can learn"
  - "Wait for their monthly one-to-one meeting in three weeks, when there is more time"
  - "Say nothing this time, because it was his first demo and he will learn on his own"
correct: 0
explanations:
  - "Right. Correction belongs in private and soon. A call helps with a serious point, and choosing a time that suits both respects the time zones."
  - "Correction in a shared channel embarrasses Bilal and does not make the message clearer."
  - "Three weeks is too long. He may show private data again in the next demo before then."
  - "Silence tells Bilal nothing was wrong. A privacy mistake is too serious to leave to chance."
```
