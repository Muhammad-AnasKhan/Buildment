## Module 2 checklist

- [ ] I can explain why vague feedback and silence both cause problems
- [ ] I can name the three parts of SBI: situation, behavior and impact
- [ ] I can turn a label such as "careless" into a description of what the person did

- [ ] I answered the questions in chapters 2.1, 2.2 and 2.3, and finished the quiz in 2.4
- [ ] I submitted my written feedback below

## Learning log

1. **Your example:** Go back to the vague feedback or the silence you described in chapter 2.1. How would SBI have changed what was said?
2. **Your habit:** When you give feedback, which do you do more often: stay silent, stay vague, or use labels? Why do you think that is?
3. **Praise:** Who on your team, in your studies or in a project deserves specific praise this week? What exactly did they do?
4. **Reviews:** Think of the last time you reviewed someone's work, or someone reviewed yours. Was it clear what had to change and what was optional?

## Your feedback

Choose a real situation: a teammate, a classmate, a volunteer or a client whose work or behavior you want to comment on. It can be praise or a correction. Change the names if you like. If you do not have a real situation, use this one:

> Your teammate Tarek demoed the new sign-up page to an NGO client yesterday. He explained each screen in simple words and stopped twice to ask if the client had questions. But he showed the page on his test account, which had real volunteer email addresses in it.
