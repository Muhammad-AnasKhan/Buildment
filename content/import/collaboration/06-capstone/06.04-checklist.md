This is the final gate of the course. Tick each box only when it is true. Your answers save as you type.

## Module 6 checklist

- [ ] I took part in a role-play session with two other fellows, or with a mentor
- [ ] I played role A (Yasmin or Mahmoud) in at least one round
- [ ] I received observer notes or mentor feedback on my round
- [ ] I submitted my reflection in chapter 6.3, with a specific change for next time
- [ ] I chose one team habit to keep and wrote it where I will see it, such as my phone or calendar

## Learning log

1. **Most useful tool:** Which tool from this course will you use most in the next month? Describe the team, client or study group where you expect to use it.
2. **Your hardest conversation:** Which is hardest for you: giving feedback, receiving criticism, disagreeing or negotiating? What is one small step to practice it?
3. **One team habit:** Choose one habit to keep, for example a daily written handoff or asking one question before you disagree. When will you do it, and what will remind you?
4. **Feedback for a partner:** Think of one person from your role-play. Write the feedback you would give them with situation, behavior, impact. Consider sending it to them.
5. **Advice to a new fellow:** If a friend starts this course tomorrow, what is one thing you would tell them?

## All boxes ticked?

Mark this chapter complete to finish the course. Your mentor will reply to your reflection with feedback and questions.

**Next:** Your next real team conversation. When it comes, use the habit you chose and notice what changes.
