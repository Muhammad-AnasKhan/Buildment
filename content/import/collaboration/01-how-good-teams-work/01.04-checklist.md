## Learning log

1. **Your team:** Go back to the team you described in chapter 1.1. Which of the three questions (owner, done and date, communication) did that team answer well, and which did it leave unclear?
2. **Unspoken expectations:** Think of one time a teammate expected something from you, or you expected something from them, and nobody said it out loud. What happened?
3. **Your messages:** Look at the last work or study message you sent that asked a question. Could someone answer it without asking you anything back? What would you add?
4. **Your hours:** When are you usually able to work, and what can change that (study, power, internet, family)? How would you tell a new team about it?

## Describe your team

```openquestion
prompt: "Describe the roles and expectations in a team you are part of now, or were part of recently: at work, in study, in a project or as a volunteer. Who owns what? What does done mean, and how are deadlines set? How does the team communicate, and how fast do people reply? Then name one thing that is unclear, and write the one-line agreement you would propose to fix it."
required: true

modelAnswer: "I volunteer with a three-person team that builds the website for a local youth NGO. Salwa owns the content, Khaled owns hosting and the domain, and I own the frontend. Deadlines come from the NGO coordinator, usually in a WhatsApp voice note, and done means the page is live on the site. We talk in a WhatsApp group and usually reply the same day, but nobody has said that out loud. The unclear part is who reviews a page before it goes live: sometimes Salwa checks it, sometimes nobody does, and twice a page went live with mistakes. I would propose: 'Before any page goes live, the person who did not build it checks it on a phone and says OK in the group.'"
```
