## Module 3 checklist

- [ ] I can name two signs of defensiveness that I notice in my own body, thoughts or words

## Learning log

1. **Your example:** Go back to the criticism you described in chapter 3.1. Which trigger was strongest for you: truth, relationship or identity?
2. **Your signs:** Did you fight back, fold or go silent? What did you notice in your body or your thoughts?
3. **The useful part:** Looking at it now, which part of the criticism was useful, even if it was not said well?
4. **Next time:** What is one thing you will do the next time you receive criticism?
