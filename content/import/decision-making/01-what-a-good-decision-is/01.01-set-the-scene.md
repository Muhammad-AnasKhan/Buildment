## A normal Tuesday

Lina is a junior developer. She works remotely for a small startup three time zones away. This morning she has three messages:

- Her team lead asks her to fix a bug before the afternoon demo.
- A freelance client asks if she can add "one small feature" by Friday.
- Her study group wants to move tomorrow's session to the evening, when power is usually back.

There is no manual for any of these. Each one is a decision. Each one has a cost, a risk and other people waiting. How she decides will shape how much her team, her client and her friends trust her.

## Why this matters for your career

When you work with an international team, people often cannot see you work. They see your choices: what you did first, what you said no to, how you explained a delay. Good judgment is one of the main reasons a remote worker gets more responsibility.

The good news: judgment is a skill. You can practice it like any other skill, with simple tools and honest reflection.

## Key words

| Word | Plain English |
|---|---|
| **Decision** | Choosing one option over others |
| **Option** | One possible choice |
| **Outcome** | What actually happens after you decide |
| **Trade-off** | Getting more of one thing means getting less of another |
| **Risk** | Something that might go wrong |
| **Bias** | A thinking habit that pushes you toward a choice without good reason |
