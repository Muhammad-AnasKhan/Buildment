This is the gate for module 1. Tick each box only when it is true. Your answers save as you type.

## Module 1 checklist

- [ ] I can explain the difference between a good decision and a good outcome
- [ ] I can give one example of a two-way door and one of a one-way door from my own life
- [ ] I can name one way to turn a one-way door into a two-way door
- [ ] I answered the questions in chapters 1.2 and 1.3

## Learning log

1. **Your decision:** Go back to the decision you described in chapter 1.1. Was it a one-way or a two-way door? Why?
2. **Quality and luck:** How did it turn out? Which parts of the result came from your choice, and which came from luck or other people?
3. **Time spent:** Did you spend the right amount of time on it, too much, or too little?
4. **Next time:** What is one thing you would do the same way, and one thing you would change?

## All boxes ticked?

Mark this chapter complete, then continue to **Trade-offs and competing options**.
