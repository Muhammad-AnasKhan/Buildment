## A promise on Monday

Ibrahim is a freelance mobile developer. On Monday, a client in Berlin called Anna asks him to update her booking app. She needs it by Thursday at 17:00 her time. On Friday morning she will show the app to investors: people who might put money into her company.

Ibrahim looks at the work. It is about 15 hours. Thursday is three days away. He says yes.

Then the week happens:

- Electricity in his area comes for about six hours a day, and the hours change every day.
- On Tuesday the internet is down for most of the afternoon.
- A build tool needs a large download that he did not plan for.

On Wednesday night he has done 9 of the 15 hours. He writes to Anna that the update will be late. She is upset. The delay is a problem, but what upsets her most is hearing about it with less than a day left, when she has no time to change her plans.

## What went wrong

Ibrahim is skilled, and he worked hard. The problem was not his effort. On Monday he planned as if the week would go perfectly. He did not ask "What could go wrong?", so he had no backup plan, and he did not warn Anna early.

None of these problems was really a surprise. Power cuts and internet outages are part of his normal week. He knew they were likely. He just did not put them into his plan or his promise.

<Callout type="info" title="This is not about blame">
Many risks in your life are not your fault and are outside your control. This module is about the part you can control: seeing risks early, preparing for them, and telling people in time.
</Callout>

## Risk and uncertainty

Two ideas run through this module.

- **Risk** is a specific thing that might go wrong, such as "the power is off for a full day".
- **Uncertainty** means you do not know enough to be sure what will happen. You may not even know every risk.

You can list risks and prepare for them. You cannot remove uncertainty, but you can still make a good decision inside it. That is what the next chapters practice.

## Key words

| Word | Plain English |
|---|---|
| **Risk** | A specific thing that might go wrong |
| **Uncertainty** | Not knowing enough to be sure what will happen |
| **Likelihood** | How probable it is that something happens |
| **Impact** | How much harm it causes if it does happen |
| **Pre-mortem** | Imagining a plan has already failed, then listing why |
| **Review date** | A day you choose in advance to check a decision again |
