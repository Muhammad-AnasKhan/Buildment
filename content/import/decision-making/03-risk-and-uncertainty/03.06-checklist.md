## Module 3 checklist

- [ ] I can rate a risk as low, medium or high for likelihood and for impact
- [ ] I can explain the two ways to make a risk smaller: less likely, or less harmful

## Learning log

1. **Your first guess:** Go back to the plan and the risk you named in chapter 3.1. After doing the pre-mortem below, was that risk still the biggest one? If not, what was?
2. **The surprise:** Which reason in your pre-mortem surprised you most? Why had you not thought of it before?
3. **What you do not know:** What is one important thing you still do not know about your plan? When will you check it again?
4. **Telling people:** Who needs to hear about your biggest risk, such as a client, a teammate or a teacher? When will you tell them?
