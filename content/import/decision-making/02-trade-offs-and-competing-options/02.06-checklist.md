## Module 2 checklist

## Learning log

1. **Your trade-off:** Go back to the choice you described in chapter 2.1. Write it as one sentence: "If I choose this, I get more of ___ and less of ___."
2. **Hidden cost:** Which cost did you not notice at first: time, energy, relationships or future options?
3. **Gut and numbers:** When you scored your options below, did the result match your first feeling? If not, what explains the difference?
4. **Limits:** Describe one decision in your life where a decision matrix would be the wrong tool. Why?
