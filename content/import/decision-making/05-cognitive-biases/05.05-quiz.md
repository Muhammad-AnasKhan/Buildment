Each scenario shows one of the four traps from this module. Read it, then choose the trap that best explains the person's thinking. Look for the clue: is it about evidence, money or time already spent, a first number, or being too sure?

```quiz
type: mcq
question: "An NGO spent 8,000 dollars and a year of training on a custom database. It is slow and staff avoid it. A free tool would do the same job better from next month. The director says: 'We cannot switch. Think of everything we put into this system.' Which trap is this?"
options:
  - "Confirmation bias"
  - "Anchoring"
  - "Sunk cost"
  - "Overconfidence"
correct: 2
explanations:
  - "The director is not filtering evidence. He accepts the system is slow. His reason is the past spending."
  - "No first number is pulling his judgment. The 8,000 dollars is a past cost, not a starting point for an estimate."
  - "Right. The money and training are spent either way. Only the future cost and value should decide."
  - "He is not claiming to be sure of an outcome. He is holding on because of what was already invested."
```

```quiz
type: mcq
question: "Mona believes her new teammate in another time zone is not committed. She notices every slow reply and mentions them in meetings. She does not mention the two nights he fixed urgent bugs before her morning. Which trap is this?"
options:
  - "Confirmation bias"
  - "Sunk cost"
  - "Overconfidence"
  - "Anchoring"
correct: 0
explanations:
  - "Right. She sees and repeats the evidence that fits her belief and leaves out the evidence against it."
  - "Nothing has been spent that she is trying to protect. The issue is which evidence she pays attention to."
  - "The clue is not a confident prediction. It is how she selects the evidence she notices."
  - "There is no first number involved. Her belief, not a number, is shaping what she sees."
```

```quiz
type: mcq
question: "Mahmoud worked out that a client's website needs about 400 dollars of work. The job post says 'Budget: 50 dollars'. Mahmoud sends a bid for 90 dollars for the full website, then feels unsure why he went so low. Which trap is this?"
options:
  - "Sunk cost"
  - "Confirmation bias"
  - "Overconfidence"
  - "Anchoring"
correct: 3
explanations:
  - "Mahmoud has not spent anything on this job yet. The problem is how he set his price."
  - "He is not searching for evidence that agrees with a belief. A single number changed his price."
  - "He is not too sure of himself. If anything, he lost confidence in his own careful estimate."
  - "Right. The 50 dollars in the post pulled his price far below his own 400 dollar estimate, though the work did not change."
```

```quiz
type: mcq
question: "Rasha has never used the framework a client wants. On the call she says: 'I am 100 percent sure I can learn it and ship the app in five days.' The last two times she learned a new tool, it took her two weeks before she was productive. Which trap is this?"
options:
  - "Anchoring"
  - "Overconfidence"
  - "Sunk cost"
  - "Confirmation bias"
correct: 1
explanations:
  - "Nobody gave her a number first. The five days came from her own feeling of certainty."
  - "Right. She feels far more certain than her experience allows, and she gives one exact number for something new."
  - "She has not invested anything yet. The problem is her certainty about the future."
  - "She is not collecting evidence that agrees with her. She is ignoring her own past experience because she feels sure."
```

```quiz
type: mcq
question: "Before a planning call, Musa wrote that a feature needs about four weeks. On the call, the team lead says first: 'This looks like two weeks to me.' When it is his turn, Musa says: 'Maybe two and a half weeks.' Nothing new was learned about the feature. Which trap is this?"
options:
  - "Overconfidence"
  - "Confirmation bias"
  - "Anchoring"
  - "Sunk cost"
correct: 2
explanations:
  - "Musa had a careful estimate and gave it up. His problem is not too much certainty."
  - "He is not filtering evidence to support a belief. He moved toward a number he heard."
  - "Right. The lead's first number pulled Musa's estimate down without any new information about the work."
  - "No past investment is involved. The feature has not been started."
```

```quiz
type: mcq
question: "Waleed believes his team's new app idea is what users want. He interviews 12 people. In his report to the founder, he quotes the 3 who liked it and calls the 9 who did not 'not our target users', though they match the target group. Which trap is this?"
options:
  - "Sunk cost"
  - "Anchoring"
  - "Overconfidence"
  - "Confirmation bias"
correct: 3
explanations:
  - "The clue is not past spending. It is how he handles the interview evidence."
  - "No first number is shaping his view. His belief is shaping which answers count."
  - "He is not making a confident prediction or estimate. The key behavior is explaining away the evidence against his belief."
  - "Right. He keeps the evidence that agrees and finds a reason to dismiss the evidence that does not."
```
