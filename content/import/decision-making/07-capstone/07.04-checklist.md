## Learning log

1. **Most useful tool:** Which tool from this course will you use most in the next month? Describe the situation where you expect to use it.
2. **Your thinking trap:** Which bias from module 5 do you fall into most often? What simple check will you use against it?
3. **A habit:** Choose one habit to keep, for example writing a short memo before any one-way door decision. When will you do it, and what will remind you?
4. **Review day:** When your review date arrives, what will you look at to judge your decision fairly, and how will you separate your reasoning from luck?
5. **Advice to a new fellow:** If a friend starts this course tomorrow, what is one thing you would tell them?
