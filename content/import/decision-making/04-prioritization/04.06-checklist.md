This is the gate for module 4. Here you use the module on your real week. Tick each box only when it is true. Your answers save as you type.

## Module 4 checklist

- [ ] I wrote down every task I need to do in the coming week, in work, study and anything else that uses my time
- [ ] I placed each task in one box of the urgent and important grid
- [ ] I marked at least one quick win, and at least one task I will drop, postpone or hand off
- [ ] I gave at least one important but not urgent task a fixed time in my week
- [ ] I wrote or sent one message to renegotiate or say no to a request I cannot fully do
- [ ] I answered the questions in chapters 4.2 to 4.5

## Learning log

1. **Your first task:** Go back to the list you wrote in chapter 4.1. Was the task you did first urgent, important, both or neither?
2. **The loud task:** Which task in your coming week sends you the most messages or pressure? Is it also the most important one?
3. **What you will not do:** What did you decide to drop or postpone? Who needs to know, and when will you tell them?
4. **Your hard part:** Saying no, guessing effort, or protecting time for important work: which is hardest for you, and why?
