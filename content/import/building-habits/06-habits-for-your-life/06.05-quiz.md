```quiz
type: mcq
question: "Rana reads one English story in the staff room after the school bell at 13:30. The bell rings at 13:30 on every school day, all year. Her friend says: 'Prayer times move with the seasons, so before summer, tie your story to the afternoon prayer instead of a clock time.' What should Rana do?"
options:
  - "Keep the 13:30 bell, since it lands at the same point all year"
  - "Move the story to after the afternoon prayer, as her friend says"
  - "Move the story to after the evening meal, which also moves"
  - "Set a phone alarm at 13:30 as well, to be extra safe"
correct: 0
explanations:
  - "That fits the course. The bell happens every school day at 13:30, in the same place, in winter and in summer. A clock time works well when the part of the day it belongs to does not move."
  - "The course does not rank event cues above clock cues; research found them about equally effective. Her bell already works, and moving the habit throws away a working cue."
  - "Her habit works at school, after the bell. The evening meal is a different part of her day, and moving there gains nothing."
  - "An alarm is a reminder, not a cue, and the bell already rings at the right moment."
```

```quiz
type: mcq
question: "Duaa now uses online lectures in her 40-minute block after lunch. In 5 of her last 6 blocks the afternoon internet was too weak to load the lecture, and she stopped after 10 minutes. The internet is usually good early in the morning. Her phone data is expensive and also weak in the afternoons. Which change fits these facts?"
options:
  - "Watch the lectures on her phone data in the afternoons instead"
  - "Study only on days when the afternoon internet is strong"
  - "Choose shorter online lectures, so they load more quickly"
  - "Download the afternoon's lecture each morning, when it loads well"
correct: 3
explanations:
  - "Her phone data is expensive and also weak in the afternoons, so this moves the same problem to a costlier place."
  - "That makes the habit depend on luck: the internet was weak on 5 of her last 6 afternoons."
  - "A shorter lecture still needs the afternoon internet that keeps failing."
  - "This is the one. The weak afternoon internet stopped 5 of her 6 blocks. A lecture saved in the morning needs no connection after lunch."
```

```quiz
type: mcq
question: "Wael often works late with clients abroad. He sleeps from about 1:30 to 7:30 on most days and from 4:00 to 12:00 on Fridays. Which plan fits the sleep advice in chapter 6.4?"
options:
  - "Keep his weekday hours and sleep longer on Fridays to catch up"
  - "Bed at 1:00 and up at 7:00, at the same times every day"
  - "Bed at 23:30 and up at 7:00, at the same times every day"
  - "Sleep whenever he feels tired, so his body can decide"
correct: 2
explanations:
  - "Six hours on most nights is less than the seven most adults need, and a very different time on Fridays is the opposite of a regular schedule."
  - "These times are regular, but they give only six hours, less than the seven hours the advice gives for most adults."
  - "Right. 23:30 to 7:00 is seven and a half hours, at the same times every day, so it meets both parts of the advice."
  - "The advice is to keep a regular time for going to bed and getting up. 'Whenever I am tired' is not regular."
```

```quiz
type: mcq
question: "Karim plans to practise spreadsheets after his afternoon nap. He waits until he feels focused before he starts, and on 4 of the last 7 days that feeling never came. Which change matches this module?"
options:
  - "Wait for days with more energy, and practise longer on those days"
  - "Set a phone alarm at 15:00 to tell him it is time to start"
  - "Practise only on Fridays, when he has the whole day free"
  - "Use a start signal after his nap, with the same chair and note"
correct: 3
explanations:
  - "Waiting for the right feeling is exactly what failed on 4 of 7 days."
  - "An alarm reminds him, but the start is still left to a decision and a feeling. A signal that always comes after his nap starts him without deciding."
  - "Once a week is far fewer repetitions, and it still leaves the start to a feeling."
  - "Yes. A start signal right after his nap makes starting the habit, so he does not need to feel focused first. Yesterday's note means no deciding what to do."
```

```quiz
type: mcq
question: "Manar wants a regular bedtime of about 23:00. She thinks of tying it to 'after the night prayer'. In this example, the night prayer is at about 18:15 in winter and about 21:15 in summer. Her family's evening tea ends at about 22:30 all year. Which anchor fits her bedtime?"
options:
  - "After the night prayer, whatever the season of the year"
  - "After the family's evening tea ends, at about 22:30"
  - "When she starts to feel sleepy, whatever the time"
  - "After she finishes watching videos on her phone"
correct: 1
explanations:
  - "The night prayer moves through the year. In winter it comes at about 18:15, far too early for a 23:00 bedtime."
  - "Yes. The tea ends at about 22:30 in every season, so a bedtime tied to it stays in the same place all year."
  - "Feeling sleepy is not a fixed point, so her bedtime would change from night to night."
  - "Videos have no fixed end, so this cue can keep moving later."
```
