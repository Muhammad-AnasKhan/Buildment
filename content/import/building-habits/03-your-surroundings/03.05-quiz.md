```quiz
type: mcq
question: "Tamer, a water delivery driver, wants to note his deliveries and cash every day. His round ends at the depot between 15:00 and 17:00, where he counts the cash in the cab. He prays at the mosque most evenings, or at his sister's home when he visits her. His phone is often flat by the afternoon. Which cue fits best?"
options:
  - "Right after he counts the cash in the cab"
  - "At 16:00 each day, when his phone alarm rings"
  - "After the evening prayer at the mosque"
  - "Whenever he finds a quiet moment in the day"
correct: 0
explanations:
  - "All four facts agree. Counting the cash happens every working day, in the same place, just before he would write. The notebook can live in the cab door, so the cue and the tool are together."
  - "His round ends at different times, so 16:00 may come in the middle of a delivery, and the phone that should ring is often flat by then."
  - "On the evenings he visits his sister, he prays at her home, so the mosque does not come every day and the notebook would not be there."
  - "A quiet moment is not a cue. Nothing in the situation starts it, and on a busy day it may never come."
```

```quiz
type: mcq
question: "Anas, a youth coach, read for 15 minutes each evening on the balcony of his old flat. Six weeks ago his family moved to a flat with no balcony, and he has read on 3 of 42 evenings since. After the evening meal, he always makes tea in the new kitchen, which has a small table. Which plan fits?"
options:
  - "Wait until the family can find a flat with a balcony again"
  - "Read in bed before sleep, now that the balcony is gone"
  - "Keep the old time, with a phone alarm after the evening meal"
  - "Read at the new kitchen table while his tea cools"
correct: 3
explanations:
  - "The balcony was the old cue, and it may not come back. Waiting leaves the habit with no cue for months, while the new routine after the meal goes unused."
  - "A new place is a good idea, but bed is a cue for sleeping, and nothing in his evening leads him there at a set moment."
  - "An alarm reminds him, but it does not give the habit a place. The balcony was part of the cue, and it is gone."
  - "Good choice. Making tea after the meal happens every evening in the new flat, and the kitchen table is right there. The habit gets a new cue and a new place, which is how habits are rebuilt after a move."
```

```quiz
type: mcq
question: "Lama, the nurse, wants to read her English book in the evenings. Reading takes 4 steps: the bag from the hall, the book, her glasses, the lamp. A series takes 1: the remote is on the sofa. Her brother also watches the family television. She read on 2 of the last 7 evenings. Which change is fair and fits this module?"
options:
  - "Hide the television remote from the family in the evenings"
  - "Keep the book, her glasses and the lamp together by her seat"
  - "Promise herself firmly to read in the evenings next week"
  - "Read only on the evenings when she feels full of energy"
correct: 1
explanations:
  - "Her brother also watches the television, so hiding the remote makes his evening harder, not only hers. In a shared home, add steps to your own choices."
  - "Good choice. With the book, glasses and lamp in one place by her seat, reading drops from 4 steps to about 1, the same as the remote. That helps most on tired evenings."
  - "A promise changes none of the steps. Reading still takes 4 and the series still takes 1, the same choice that gave 2 of 7."
  - "High-energy evenings are the ones where steps matter least. Her missed evenings are the tired ones, so this keeps the result the same."
```

```quiz
type: mcq
question: "Iyad, the computer science student, has a second habit: after the dawn prayer he reviews 10 programming terms on a flashcard app. The app needs the internet, which is often off at dawn. On some weeks his phone is also flat at dawn. He keeps a paper notebook by his bed. Which backup version fits these facts?"
options:
  - "Review the cards in the app later, once the internet is back"
  - "Keep a power bank charged so his phone is ready at dawn"
  - "Read five terms from a paper list beside his prayer mat"
  - "Skip the habit on mornings when the internet is off"
correct: 2
explanations:
  - "This moves the habit away from its cue, the dawn prayer, and it still depends on the internet, which is the main problem."
  - "A charged phone solves the flat battery but not the missing internet, so the app still cannot open his cards on many mornings."
  - "Right. A paper list needs no power and no internet, and it stays at the same cue. He can copy the terms from the app into his notebook on a good day."
  - "With no backup, every morning without internet is a morning without the habit, and on some weeks that is most mornings."
```

```quiz
type: mcq
question: "Sara's habit on her walk home: full, a downloaded 15-minute lesson; backup, her word card; smallest, three new words aloud. Last week: two days full; three days backup, each noted 'lesson not downloaded'; one day smallest, noted 'very tired'. This week the power is on from 20:00 to 04:00. Which change fits her week?"
options:
  - "Download the lessons during the evening power hours"
  - "Drop the backup version, since she used it on three days"
  - "Make the smallest version longer, since Friday went fine"
  - "Use the full version only in weeks with daytime power"
correct: 0
explanations:
  - "The notes agree. Three of her short days say the same thing: the lesson was not downloaded. The power is on every evening this week, so a download in those hours makes the full version possible on most days."
  - "The backup did its job: it kept the habit going on three days. Without it, those days would have been misses."
  - "The smallest-version day was noted 'very tired'. The smallest version is for exactly that kind of day, so making it bigger would make it easier to skip."
  - "She does not need daytime power. The lessons can download in the evening power hours, so waiting would cost her full lessons."
```
