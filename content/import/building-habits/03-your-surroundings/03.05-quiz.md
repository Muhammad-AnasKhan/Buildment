```quiz
type: mcq
question: "Tamer, the water delivery driver, wants to write his deliveries and cash in a notebook every day. Four facts: his round ends between 13:00 and 16:00; before driving home he always counts the cash in the cab; he goes to the mosque for the evening prayer on most days, but not when he visits his sister; his phone is often flat by the afternoon. Which cue fits best?"
options:
  - "Right after he counts the cash in the cab each day"
  - "At 14:00 each day, when an alarm on his phone goes off"
  - "After the evening prayer, once he is back at home"
  - "Whenever the round ends early enough to leave him time"
correct: 0
explanations:
  - "Right. Counting the cash happens every working day, in the same place, and it is the action just before writing. The notebook can live in the cab door, so the cue and the tool are together."
  - "His round ends at different times, so 14:00 may come in the middle of a delivery, and the phone that should ring is often flat."
  - "He skips the evening prayer at the mosque on the days he visits his sister, so this cue does not happen every day."
  - "'Early enough' is not a cue. Nothing in the situation starts it, and his end time changes from day to day."
```

```quiz
type: mcq
question: "Sara, the tailor's assistant, gets home at 19:00. She drops her bag on her bed, lies down 'for a minute', picks up her phone, and an hour goes by. She wants 10 minutes of English pronunciation practice each evening. Which change works on the earliest link she controls?"
options:
  - "Put the phone down after 15 minutes of scrolling in bed"
  - "Leave her bag by the door, beside her lesson sheet"
  - "Practise in bed after she has rested there for an hour"
  - "Turn off the phone's sound before she lies down to rest"
correct: 1
explanations:
  - "This fights the last link, when the chain is already running and she is lying down with the phone in her hand."
  - "Right. Dropping the bag on the bed is the first link, and it leads to lying down. Leaving the bag by the door, next to the lesson sheet, keeps her away from the bed and gives her a new cue before the old chain starts."
  - "After an hour in bed with the phone, the old chain has already run. This keeps every link that leads to scrolling."
  - "A silent phone is still in her hand once she lies down. The bed and the phone remain the next links in her chain."
```

```quiz
type: mcq
question: "Lama, the nurse, wants to read her English book in the evenings. In this example, reading takes 4 steps: get the bag from the hall, find the book, find her glasses, switch on the lamp. Watching a series takes 1 step: the remote is on the sofa. Her brother also watches the family television. She read on 2 of the last 7 evenings. Which change follows this module's advice and is fair in a shared home?"
options:
  - "Hide the television remote from the whole family every evening"
  - "Promise herself to read on all 7 evenings next week"
  - "Read only on the evenings when she feels full of energy"
  - "Keep the book, glasses and lamp together by her seat"
correct: 3
explanations:
  - "Her brother also watches the television, so hiding the remote makes his evening harder, not only hers. In a shared home, add friction to your own choices."
  - "A promise changes none of the steps. Reading still takes 4 steps and the series still takes 1, the same choice that gave 2 of 7."
  - "High-energy evenings are the ones where steps matter least. Her missed evenings are the tired ones, so this would keep the result the same."
  - "Right. With the book, glasses and lamp in one place by her seat, reading drops from 4 steps to about 1, the same as the remote. That helps most on tired evenings."
```

```quiz
type: mcq
question: "Iyad, the computer science student, reviews 10 programming terms on a flashcard app after the dawn prayer. The app needs the internet to open his cards, and the internet is often off at dawn. On weeks with night power, his phone is charged; on other weeks it is often flat. He keeps a paper notebook by his bed. Which backup version fits these facts?"
options:
  - "Review the same cards in the app later, once the internet is back"
  - "Skip the habit on the mornings when his phone is flat"
  - "Read five terms from a paper list kept beside his prayer mat"
  - "Keep a power bank charged so the phone is never flat at dawn"
correct: 2
explanations:
  - "This moves the habit away from its cue, the dawn prayer, and it still depends on the internet, which is the problem."
  - "With no backup, every flat-phone morning is a morning without the habit, and on some weeks that is most mornings."
  - "Right. A paper list needs no power and no internet, and it stays at the same cue. He can copy the terms from the app into his notebook on a good day."
  - "A charged phone does not help when the internet is off at dawn. The app still cannot open his cards."
```

Last week: Sunday and Monday full; Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday backup, each noted 'lesson not downloaded'; Friday smallest, noted 'very tired'. This week the power at home is on from 20:00 to 04:00. Which one change does her tracker support?"

```quiz
  - "Download the next lessons during the evening power hours"
  - "Drop the backup version, since she used it too often"
  - "Make the smallest version longer, since Friday went fine"
  - "Use the full version only in weeks with daytime power"
correct: 0
explanations:
  - "Right. Three of her short days have the same note: the lesson was not downloaded. The power is on every evening this week, so a download in those hours makes the full version possible on most days."
  - "The backup did its job: it kept the habit going on three days. Without it, those days would have been misses."
  - "Friday's note says 'very tired'. The smallest version is for exactly that kind of day, so making it bigger would make it easier to skip."
  - "She does not need daytime power. The lessons can download in the evening power hours, so waiting would cost her full lessons."
```
