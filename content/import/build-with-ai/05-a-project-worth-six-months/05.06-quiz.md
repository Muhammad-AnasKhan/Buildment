```quiz
type: mcq
question: "Lama is building a booking app for her sports club. She has 6 hours a week for 6 months, and her project has 9 areas. She asks a chat assistant for a schedule, and it answers: 'With AI tools, each area takes about 2 hours, so all 9 areas fit in your first 3 weeks.' What is the main mistake in this answer?"
options:
  - "Its sum is wrong: 9 areas at 2 hours is more than 3 weeks"
  - "It counts only building time and leaves out the learning"
  - "It uses too many hours a week; she only has 6 to spend"
  - "It plans 9 areas, but a club app could never need that many"
correct: 1
explanations:
  - "Nine areas at 2 hours is 18 hours, and 3 weeks at 6 hours is also 18 hours. The arithmetic is fine; what it counts is the problem."
  - "Right. Two hours an area is a building estimate with AI help. Learning an area takes much longer, and the answer has no learning hours at all. Her 6 months give 6 times 4 times 6, which is 144 hours, room for both."
  - "The answer used her 6 hours a week correctly. The hours a week are not where it went wrong."
  - "Nothing in the facts says 9 areas is too many. The number of areas is Lama's decision, and the answer accepted it."
```

```quiz
type: mcq
question: "Duaa, from the coding bootcamp, has 5 hours a week for her print shop order page. Her plan for week 5 says: new areas, file uploads and payments. Resources: two videos and one article, all opened and checked. Last day: a recall test with her notes closed. Hours: 5, as in her plan. Which change does this course's weekly plan call for?"
options:
  - "Open the two videos and the article to check they really exist"
  - "Replace the recall test with a rewatch of both videos"
  - "Raise the week to 10 hours so that both areas can fit"
  - "Move payments to a later week, so the week has one new area"
correct: 3
explanations:
  - "She already opened and checked all three, so the 'I checked it exists' column is done."
  - "The recall test is the right way to end the week. Rewatching feels like learning, but closing the notes and recalling shows the gaps."
  - "Her plan says 5 hours, and planning with hours she does not have makes the plan fall behind in the first month."
  - "Right. Two new areas in one week break the one-new-area rule, and each area gets half her attention. Payments can be the new area of a later week."
```

```quiz
type: mcq
question: "Saleh, building an ordering app for a café, checks three resources an AI suggested for his week on user logins. The video: opened, 20 minutes, posted 8 years ago, showing commands for an old version of his framework. The article: opened, on the framework's official site, updated this year. The book: found in a bookshop listing, published 6 years ago, mostly ideas and few commands. Which source should he follow for the commands he types?"
options:
  - "The article on the official site, updated this year"
  - "The video, since it shows every command step by step"
  - "The book, since a printed book is checked more than a site"
  - "Any of the three, since he checked that all three exist"
correct: 0
explanations:
  - "Right. Commands change between versions, and the official documentation for his version is the place to check them. The video and the book can still help with the ideas."
  - "The video shows commands for an old version of the framework. Showing every step does not help if the steps have changed."
  - "The book is real and useful for ideas, but it is 6 years old and has few commands. Being printed does not make it current."
  - "Checking that a resource exists earns it a place in the plan. It does not make every resource current or right for commands."
```

```quiz
type: mcq
question: "Nizar, a freelance developer, is building a weekly feedback page for a coding bootcamp. The facts: 25 students answer once a week, the staff already edit a shared spreadsheet every day, and the budget is 10 hours of work. A chat assistant suggests a new database with its own admin page. The other option is to save answers into the staff's spreadsheet. Which option do the facts support?"
options:
  - "The database, because it can grow to thousands of students later"
  - "The database, because the staff will surely want a new admin page"
  - "The spreadsheet: it fits 25 students and the staff's daily use"
  - "The database, because the AI's suggestion comes from more data"
correct: 2
explanations:
  - "Nothing in the facts says the bootcamp will grow to thousands. With 25 weekly answers, growth should carry little weight."
  - "The staff already edit the spreadsheet every day. A new admin page means new things for them to learn, which is a cost, not a gain."
  - "Right. Twenty-five weekly answers is a small load, the staff already work in the spreadsheet, and 10 hours favours the simpler build. Nizar should write down what it gives up and what would make him change."
  - "The AI did not know this bootcamp's facts when it answered. A suggestion is only as good as the context it was given."
```

```quiz
type: mcq
question: "Adam, still building the online shop for his uncle's spice business, finds a product-photo function in a project under the Apache 2.0 licence. He wants to reuse it and change 10 of its 30 lines. What does the licence ask of him?"
options:
  - "Nothing, because Apache 2.0 is a permissive licence"
  - "Keep the notices and state that he changed the code"
  - "Share his whole shop's source under Apache 2.0 too"
  - "Ask the author first, as with code that has no licence"
correct: 1
explanations:
  - "Permissive means few conditions, not none. Apache 2.0 still asks him to keep the notices and say what he changed."
  - "Right. Apache 2.0 lets him reuse and change the code if he keeps its notices and states his changes. A credit line in his README is the course's extra habit."
  - "Sharing the whole source under the same licence is what GPL asks when you distribute. Apache 2.0 does not ask that."
  - "Asking first is for code with no licence. This project has a licence that already gives permission, with conditions."
```
