```quiz
type: mcq
question: "Rami is a freelance web developer. A client's site crashed. The client's office manager pasted the server log into a chat assistant and sent Rami its summary. 1) The log holds the site's payment API key on line 12. 2) The summary says: 'The crash began at 14:05 when the payment service timed out.' 3) The log itself shows the first error at 13:52, in the database connection. What should Rami do first?"
options:
  - "Fix the payment service, since the summary says it failed at 14:05"
  - "Get the key replaced and check the summary against the log, as both failed"
  - "Delete the chat with the log, since that takes the key out of the tool"
  - "Paste the log into a second AI tool, since two summaries are safer"
correct: 1
explanations:
  - "The log puts the first error at 13:52, in the database connection, before any payment timeout. The summary is wrong, and the key is still exposed."
  - "Right. Two habits at once. A key that went into a chat tool is treated as stolen: cancel it and make a new one. And an AI summary is checked against its source, which here points to the database at 13:52."
  - "Deleting the chat feels like a fix, but the key has already left the client's hands. Treat it as stolen and replace it. The summary also still needs checking."
  - "A second tool would spread the key further, and a second summary is still not the source. The log itself already answers the question."
```

```quiz
type: mcq
question: "Ola builds a small order app for a tailor's shop and posts a weekly write-up. 1) A chat assistant wrote the first draft of this week's post from her log, and she rewrote about half of it. 2) She checked every number against her log. 3) The screenshot uses made-up customers. 4) Her AI-use line says: 'AI fixed my grammar.' What should she change before posting?"
options:
  - "The screenshot, since it shows the tailor's real customers"
  - "The numbers, since an AI draft may have changed one of them"
  - "Nothing, since she rewrote half and checked every number"
  - "The line, since AI drafted the post, not just its grammar"
correct: 3
explanations:
  - "Fact 3 says the customers in the screenshot are made up, so it is already safe to share."
  - "Checking numbers after an AI draft is the right habit, and fact 2 says she has already done it."
  - "Her rewriting and checking are real work, but the line still tells readers less than what happened. It must say that AI wrote the first draft."
  - "Right. The line must match what AI did: it wrote the first draft, and she rewrote and checked it. 'AI fixed my grammar' hides most of its part."
```

```quiz
type: mcq
question: "Firas is preparing for a databases exam at his university. 1) He read a chat assistant's explanation of transactions three times, and it felt clear. 2) With the page closed, he answered 2 of the 6 questions he wrote for himself. 3) The exam is in 12 days. What is his best next step?"
options:
  - "Study the 4 gaps and test again later, as recall found them"
  - "Ask for a simpler explanation, since the first one did not stick"
  - "Read it a fourth time, since each reading felt clearer than the last"
  - "Move on to the next topic, since the explanation now feels easy"
correct: 0
explanations:
  - "Right. Recall showed exactly what he does not know yet: 4 of 6. He studies only those, then recalls again after a few days, and there are 12 days to do it."
  - "A clearer explanation can help with one gap, but it is more reading. Reading clearly is what made him feel he knew it while he answered only 2 of 6."
  - "Each reading felt clearer, and that feeling is the trap. With the page closed he answered only 2 of 6."
  - "Feeling easy is not knowing. His own test says 2 of 6, so the topic is not done."
```

```quiz
type: mcq
question: "Tamer plans a lending app for a school library. 1) He has 6 hours a week for 2 months, which is 8 weeks. 2) The app has 10 areas, and he plans 10% of his time for learning. 3) His own estimate is about 6 hours to learn an area and 3 hours to build it with AI help. What does the arithmetic tell him?"
options:
  - "The plan works, since AI can write the code for all 10 areas"
  - "Learn each area after the app is done, since building comes first"
  - "Add months and learning, since 48 hours cannot cover 90"
  - "Add two more areas, since a bigger app impresses reviewers more"
correct: 2
explanations:
  - "Even if AI did all the building, his own estimate puts learning at 60 hours. At 10% of 48 hours, he has under 5 hours for learning."
  - "He would have an app he cannot explain. A reviewer's first 'why?' would find the gap, as it did for Khaled in module 5."
  - "Right. 6 hours a week for 8 weeks is 48 hours, and 10 areas need about 90: 60 for learning and 30 for building. A longer plan with a larger learning share fits."
  - "More areas need even more hours, and the plan is already too short. A project you understand beats a bigger one."
```

```quiz
type: mcq
question: "Amira volunteers at an NGO office that runs a winter clothes day for 300 families. She told a chat assistant: '300 families, 4 volunteers, one day.' It wrote: 'Open the hall at 9:00. Serve families in order of arrival. Each of the 4 volunteers serves 75 families.' Amira knows a school uses the hall until 13:00. What should she do first?"
options:
  - "Ask for a longer plan with a time for every step, since detail fixes gaps"
  - "Ask it to share the 300 families out again, since 75 each is a lot"
  - "Post the plan now, since the opening time can be fixed on the day itself"
  - "Add the hall's hours and reread the plan, as the AI lacked them"
correct: 3
explanations:
  - "A longer plan built on the wrong opening time is still wrong. More detail cannot replace a fact the AI was never given."
  - "The sum is right: 300 families shared by 4 volunteers is 75 each. The problem is the opening time, which the AI could not know."
  - "Families would arrive at 9:00 at a hall the school is using until 13:00. Checking the plan is Amira's call, and it comes before posting, not on the day."
  - "Right. The plan's mistake comes from a fact only Amira knew: the hall is busy until 13:00. She adds that context, and she still reads and decides on the new plan herself."
```
