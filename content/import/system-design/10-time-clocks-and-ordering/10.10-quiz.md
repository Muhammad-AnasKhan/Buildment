```quiz
type: mcq
question: "A service gives up on a request after 2 seconds, measured as Date.now() at the end minus Date.now() at the start. During a request, a time sync steps the host clock back by 3 seconds. What happens, and what is the fix?"
options:
  - "It fires 3 seconds early; sync clocks more often"
  - "It fires 3 seconds early; use a monotonic clock"
  - "It fires 3 seconds late; use a monotonic clock"
  - "Nothing changes: a sync moves the date, not elapsed time"
correct: 2
explanations:
  - "The direction is wrong. The end reading is 3 seconds smaller, so the measured time is too small and the timeout fires late. More syncs also mean more steps, not fewer."
  - "The fix is right, but the direction is wrong. A clock stepped back makes the difference smaller, so the timeout waits longer than intended, not shorter."
  - "Right. The measured time is 3 seconds too small, so the timeout fires 3 seconds late. A monotonic clock only moves forward and ignores steps."
  - "Elapsed time computed from the wall clock moves with every step. The date and the difference come from the same clock, so both change."
```

```quiz
type: mcq
question: "Two edits to one document carry Lamport stamps 7 and 5, and vectors [3,1,0] and [1,0,2]. A teammate says the edit with stamp 5 came first. What do you answer?"
options:
  - "The vectors cross, so the edits are concurrent"
  - "Right: 5 is less than 7, so that edit came first"
  - "The vector with the bigger sum came first"
  - "Nobody can tell, because the clocks disagree"
correct: 0
explanations:
  - "Right. [3,1,0] leads in the first slot and [1,0,2] leads in the third, so neither is at most the other. The edits are concurrent, and the smaller stamp proves nothing."
  - "A smaller Lamport number is not proof of an earlier cause. Only the reverse is guaranteed: a cause always has the smaller number."
  - "The sum of a vector has no meaning for order. Only a slot-by-slot comparison tells before, after or concurrent."
  - "Vector clocks do not use physical clocks at all. The vectors give a definite answer: the edits are concurrent."
```

```quiz
type: mcq
question: "Node B's hybrid stamp is (200, 4) and its own clock reads 150. A message stamped (180, 9) arrives. What is B's stamp afterwards?"
options:
  - "(200, 10)"
  - "(180, 10)"
  - "(150, 0)"
  - "(200, 5)"
correct: 3
explanations:
  - "That takes the message's counter, 9, and adds 1. The message's time, 180, is smaller than B's 200, so B's own counter is the one that counts on."
  - "That adopts the message's time and counter. The new time is the biggest of 200, 180 and 150, which is 200, not 180."
  - "That follows the node's own clock, which is behind. The new stamp must be bigger than B's old stamp (200, 4)."
  - "Right. The biggest time is 200, from B's own stamp alone, so the counter counts on from B's own 4 to 5."
```

  - "This keeps new rows at the end, but it needs one central counter, which the question rules out, and it is a single point of failure."
