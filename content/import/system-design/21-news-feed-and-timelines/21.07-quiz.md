```quiz
question: "Mahal (a made-up app) has 20 million daily users. Each opens the feed 5 times a day and posts 0.1 times a day. The busiest second is 3 times the average, and a day has about 86,400 seconds. About how many feed opens and posts a second does it serve at the peak?"

  - "About 1,200 feed opens and 23 posts a second"

  - "About 3,500 feed opens and 7 posts a second"

  - "Right. 20,000,000 x 5 = 100,000,000 opens a day, about 1,157 a second, and x 3 is about 3,500. 20,000,000 x 0.1 = 2,000,000 posts a day, about 23 a second, and x 3 is about 70."
  - "The feed opens are right, but the posts are ten times too low. 2,000,000 posts a day is about 23 a second, which is about 70 at the peak."
```

```quiz
options:
  - "More than 3 times, so a long backlog builds"
  - "About a third, so nothing else is delayed"
  - "Exactly all of it, with no spare capacity left"
  - "About 3%, because posts are sent one at a time"
correct: 0
explanations:
  - "Right. 4 posts x 5,000,000 followers is 20,000,000 writes. One minute of the fleet is 100,000 x 60 = 6,000,000, so the work is more than 3 times the capacity and about 14 million writes are still waiting after the minute."
  - "This turns the ratio upside down. The work is 20 million writes against 6 million of capacity, so it is 3.3 times too big, not a third."
  - "That would need the work to be 6 million writes. Each post alone is 5 million, so 4 posts are far more than the minute can hold."
  - "Sending posts one at a time does not shrink the work. Every post is still 5 million writes, so the fleet's minute is far exceeded."
```

```quiz
type: mcq
question: "In the hybrid, a reader's feed open makes 7 lookups: her own timeline and 6 star lists. Each lookup is slow 1% of the time, independently. About what share of her feed opens meet at least one slow lookup?"
options:
  - "About 1%"
  - "About 49%"
  - "About 63%"
  - "About 7%"
correct: 3
explanations:
  - "That is the chance for a single lookup. With 7 lookups there are 7 chances for something to be slow."
  - "This multiplies 7 by 7. The chance is 1 minus 0.99 to the power of 7, which is much smaller."
  - "That is the figure for 100 lookups (1 minus 0.99 to the power of 100). Seven lookups give far fewer chances."
  - "Right. The chance that all 7 are fast is 0.99 to the power of 7, about 0.932, so about 6.8% of feed opens meet a slow one."
```

```quiz
type: mcq
```
```quiz
question: "The timeline cache restarts empty at the busiest moment, and the timeline store is fine. About 17,000 feed opens a second now miss the cache. What is the safest way to recover?"
options:
  - "Return empty feeds until the cache has warmed up"
  - "Rebuild all 50 million timelines before serving anyone"
  - "Reload each reader's list from the store on a miss"
  - "Send every miss to pull and build lists from scratch"
correct: 2
explanations:
  - "Readers would see no posts for minutes. An empty feed looks like a broken app, and the data is safe in the store."
  - "Nothing needs rebuilding, because the store still holds every timeline. Waiting for a full rebuild would keep everyone out for no reason."
  - "Right. Each miss is one read of one list from the store. 17,000 reads a second of different lists spread over many partitions is well within what the store can serve, and the cache refills as it goes."
  - "A pull build is about 150 lookups. At 17,000 misses a second that is about 2.6 million lookups a second, which is the overload the timelines were built to avoid."
```
