This is the gate for module 1. Tick each box only when it is true. Your answers save as you type.

## Module 1 checklist

- [ ] I can explain the difference between a task, its output and its outcome
- [ ] I can name three habits of owning the outcome: ask why, check where it is used, close the loop
- [ ] I can name the four parts of a mistake report: what happened, impact, fix and what changes
- [ ] I wrote a mistake report in chapter 1.3
- [ ] I answered the questions in chapters 1.1, 1.2 and 1.3, and finished the quiz in 1.4

## Learning log

1. **Your task:** Go back to the task you described in chapter 1.1. Did you own the outcome fully, or did you stop at the task? What tells you that?
2. **The outcome:** Who was that task really for, and what did they need from it? Did your work give them that?
3. **What you checked:** What did you check before you called it done? What one extra check would have told you more?
4. **A mistake:** Think of a mistake you made at work, in study or in a project. How long did it take you to tell someone, and why?
5. **Next time:** Which one habit from this module will you use on your next task, and how will you remember to use it?
