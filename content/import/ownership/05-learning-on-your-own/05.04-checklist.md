## Module 5 checklist

- [ ] I chose one new tool or topic I need for my work, study or a project
- [ ] I found its official docs and checked which version I will use
- [ ] I saved the docs, or the pages I need, so I can read them offline
- [ ] I started a notes file for this tool, with at least three lines in it
- [ ] I wrote a help question using the template: goal, what I tried, exact error, what I expected
- [ ] I answered the questions in chapters 5.2 and 5.3

## Learning log

1. **Looking back:** Go back to the tool or topic you described in chapter 5.1. Which of the three pieces (docs, a tiny project, notes) did you use, and which did you miss?
2. **Your time-box:** How long do you usually try alone before you ask for help? Is that too long, too short or about right?
3. **Offline days:** What will you do during your next online hours, so you can keep learning when the internet is off?
4. **Asking for help:** What stops you from asking for help sooner, or makes you ask too early? What will you do differently?
