## Learning log

1. **Your first reaction:** Go back to the change you described in chapter 6.1. Looking at it now, which question would you ask first about the reason behind it?
2. **Inside and outside:** Think of a recent setback. Which parts were outside your control? Is there one small thing within your control for next time, or was there nothing you could have done?
3. **Your support:** Who are two people you could ask for help when things go wrong: one for practical help and one to talk to?
4. **A pattern you plan around:** Is there a setback that keeps happening for you, like power cuts or a busy exam period? What is one way you could plan around it?
