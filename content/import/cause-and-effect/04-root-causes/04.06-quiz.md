```quiz
type: mcq
question: "Ward works at a laundry shop that promises clothes 'ready in one day'. Last week, 14 of 50 orders were late. All 14 came in after 16:00. From 16:00, one worker irons and also serves the counter. Late customers now get a small discount. Which root cause should he check first?"
options:
  - "The discount for late orders is not generous enough yet"
  - "A one-day promise is far too short for any laundry to keep"
  - "The worker irons too slowly in the late afternoon"
  - "After 16:00, one person irons and serves the counter"
correct: 3
explanations:
  - "The discount is a symptom fix. A bigger one might calm customers, but the orders would still be late."
  - "Orders that came in before 16:00 were ready on time, so the promise can be kept. Something changes at 16:00."
  - "This blames a person. From 16:00 the worker is doing two jobs at once. The cause is in how the work is shared, not in her speed."
  - "Right. The late orders are exactly the ones that arrive when one person has two jobs. That is a step the shop can change, for example with a second person at the counter from 16:00."
```

```quiz
type: mcq
question: "Dalia's car wash: last week 6 of 40 cars left with dirty wheels. Why 1: workers skip wheels when busy. Why 2: the brush is kept in the back room. Why 3: there is no hook for it at the bay. Why 4: the bay was built before they washed wheels. A hook costs 20 shekels. Rebuilding the bay costs 4,000 and closes it for a month. Where should she fix first?"
options:
  - "At the hook: hang the brush right at the washing bay"
  - "At the workers: remind them not to skip the wheels"
  - "At the bay: rebuild it so wheels are part of the design"
  - "At the back room: tidy it so the brush is easy to find"
correct: 0
explanations:
  - "Right. The missing hook is a process cause the car wash can change today, cheaply. Once the brush hangs at the bay, the deeper why no longer matters: the fix blocks it."
  - "This is a reminder, a weak fix, and it stops the chain at the workers. The next why already shows what makes skipping easy: the brush is far away."
  - "This is a design change, but it costs 4,000 shekels and a month without the bay. The deepest why is not always the best place to fix. A 20 shekel hook blocks the same chain today."
  - "A tidy back room still leaves the brush away from the bay. Busy workers would still have to walk to fetch it."
```

```quiz
type: mcq
question: "Manar volunteers at a school library, where 18 books went missing last term. Her checks: 12 of the 18 were last seen on the shelf by the door. The sign-out sheet was used every day. None were in the cleaner's room. The librarian cannot see that shelf from her desk. What do the checks support?"
options:
  - "Missing sign-out sheets on some days are the main cause"
  - "Books leave unseen from the shelf the desk cannot see"
  - "The cleaner moving books is the main cause of the losses"
  - "All four causes on the fishbone played an equal part"
correct: 1
explanations:
  - "The sheet was used every day, so there were no missing sheets."
  - "Right. Two checks fit together: most losses came from the one shelf the librarian cannot see. A fix could be as simple as moving that shelf into view of the desk."
  - "The books were not in the cleaner's room. This cause was checked and did not hold."
  - "Two thirds of the losses came from one shelf. The causes were not equal, and a fishbone does not make them equal."
```

```quiz
type: mcq
question: "Osama runs a food cart by a university gate. He ran out of bread before 13:00 on 8 of 20 days, so he started ordering from a written list. In the next 20 days he ran out on 4. On all 4, the baker came after 10:00 instead of 7:00. What do the facts support?"
options:
  - "The list fixed nothing, since he still ran out on some days"
  - "He should go back to ordering from memory, as before"
  - "The 4 days were bad luck, so there is nothing left to find"
  - "There is a second cause: the baker's late deliveries"
correct: 3
explanations:
  - "The empty days fell from 8 to 4, and every remaining one was a late-delivery day. The list seems to help, though one period of 20 days is a hint, not proof."
  - "On every day with an early delivery, the list kept him supplied. Going back would bring back the first cause."
  - "All 4 empty days share one pattern: a late delivery. A repeated pattern is a cause to follow, not luck."
  - "Right. The remaining empty days all point to a second branch: late deliveries. Problems often have more than one root cause, and this one needs its own whys."
```

```quiz
type: mcq
question: "Hala's family runs a small butcher's shop. Three times this year, the freezer door was left open overnight and meat was spoiled. Each time, a different person had closed the shop. Which fix is the strongest?"
options:
  - "A sign on the freezer saying 'close the door before you leave'"
  - "Tell the evening staff that this is their final warning"
  - "Fit a door that swings shut by itself when let go"
  - "Add 'check the freezer' to the closing checklist"
correct: 2
explanations:
  - "A sign is a warning, one of the weaker fixes. It asks a tired person to remember at the end of the day."
  - "A warning blames people, and it happened with three different people. The cause is in the setup, not in one person."
  - "Right. A door that closes by itself changes the design, so nobody has to remember. That is a strong fix."
  - "A checklist is a middle fix. It helps, but it still depends on someone remembering to use it properly at closing time."
```
