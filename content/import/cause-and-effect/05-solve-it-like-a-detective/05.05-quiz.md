```quiz
type: mcq
question: "Aseel's school canteen has two tills. (1) Since Sunday, till 2's daily total is about 40 shekels below its cash. (2) Before Sunday, it matched. (3) Till 1 still matches. (4) On Sunday the sandwich price rose from 5 to 6 shekels; a helper changed it on till 2. Which conclusion fits?"
options:
  - "The new price list is wrong, so correct it on both of the tills"
  - "Someone at till 2 is taking money out of the drawer each day"
  - "Till 2 is old now and has started to add up its totals wrongly"
  - "Till 2 may still have the old sandwich price; check that first"
correct: 3
explanations:
  - "Fact 3 rules this out: till 1 uses the new price list and matches its drawer every day."
  - "Fact 1 rules this out: the drawer has more cash than the total, not less. Taking money would leave less."
  - "Fact 2 makes this weak: till 2 matched every day before Sunday. An old till would not start to fail on the same day as the price change for no reason."
  - "Right. Only till 2 is affected, it started on Sunday, and the drawer has more cash than the till recorded. A till still charging 5 shekels while customers pay 6 explains all of that."
```

```quiz
type: mcq
question: "Bushra's coffee stall has two machines. (1) Since Monday, machine 1's coffee is bitter. (2) It is bitter with or without milk. (3) It started with a new bag of coffee. (4) Machine 2 is fine with the same water. (5) A test cup takes 3 minutes; a repair visit costs 150 shekels. Best next check?"
options:
  - "Book a repair visit for machine 1 this week, just to be safe"
  - "Make one cup on machine 1 with coffee from the old bag"
  - "Change the water tank that both of the machines use first"
  - "Serve only milky drinks from machine 1 for the time being"
correct: 1
explanations:
  - "Fact 5 makes this a poor first move: it costs 150 shekels, while a 3-minute check of a likely cause is still untried."
  - "Right. The taste changed with the new bag (fact 3), and a test cup takes 3 minutes (fact 5). It is a cheap check of a likely cause."
  - "Fact 4 makes the water unlikely: machine 2 is fine with water from the same tank."
  - "Fact 2 rules this out: the coffee is bitter with milk too, so milky drinks will not hide the problem."
```

```quiz
type: mcq
question: "Dalal's food stall takes about 50 phone orders a day. Each order passes through 8 steps, and she can check the result of any step. Some orders arrive wrong. The result of step 4, the kitchen ticket, is correct. What should she check next?"
options:
  - "Step 2, to be sure every call was heard and noted right"
  - "Steps 5 to 8 together, so the whole check is done in one go"
  - "Step 8 again, because the wrong orders show up at that step"
  - "The result after step 6, the middle of the steps still left"
correct: 3
explanations:
  - "The correct ticket at step 4 already shows that steps 1 to 4 worked, including the call. Checking step 2 again rules out nothing new."
  - "Checking four steps together makes the answer unclear if something is wrong. One clear check in the middle tells her which half to search."
  - "She already knows the result at step 8 is wrong. Checking it again rules out nothing, while the middle rules out half of what is left."
  - "Right. Steps 5 to 8 are left. Checking the result after step 6 rules out half of them, whatever the answer is."
```

```quiz
type: mcq
question: "Fares, a bookkeeper, has a laptop that works plugged in but switches off when unplugged. An AI chat tool says: 'This is a power fault. The main board controls all the power, so replacing it is the surest fix. One repair covers the battery, the port and the power control.' What is the mistake?"
options:
  - "It picks a costly fix for everything before a test narrows it"
  - "None: one repair that covers every part is the safest choice"
  - "It should first tell him to update his software and restart"
  - "It should say to replace the charger, as power comes from there"
correct: 0
explanations:
  - "Right. His description already points at the battery side: it works plugged in and fails on battery. A repair shop can test the battery before anyone replaces the most costly part."
  - "Covering every part sounds safe, but it means paying for the most expensive repair without knowing the cause. A cheaper check could find it first."
  - "An update and a restart change many things at once and do not test his description. It also delays the real check."
  - "The laptop works when plugged in, so the charger is delivering power. The charger is not a likely cause."
```

```quiz
type: mcq
question: "Luay sells vegetables from a cart to about 60 customers a day, with a card reader linked to his phone. This morning the reader stopped connecting. He restarted it, and it worked for one payment. What should he do before he calls it fixed?"
options:
  - "Buy a new card reader so that the fault cannot come back"
  - "Ask customers to pay in cash for the rest of this month"
  - "Take a few more card payments today and note any failures"
  - "Nothing, because it connected the first time after the restart"
correct: 2
explanations:
  - "A new reader costs money before he knows whether the old one is faulty. A few more payments can tell him that for free."
  - "This avoids the problem instead of checking it, and some customers may not have cash ready."
  - "Right. One good payment may be luck, because some faults come and go. A few more payments and a short note tell him whether the fix holds."
  - "One success is not enough to confirm a fix. A fault that comes and goes can return an hour later."
```
